## Chapter 2: A Picture Is Not a Job

**VO-C02-B01**

**VO-C02-B02**

**VO-C02-B03**

**VO-C02-B04**

**VO-C02-B05**

**VO-C02-B07**

## Chapter 3: The Palace and the Alternative

**VO-C03-B01**

**VO-C03-B02**

**VO-C03-B03**

**VO-C03-B04**

**VO-C03-B08**

**VO-C03-B09**

## Chapter 5: What the Evidence Supports

**VO-C05-B02**
