The one decision that shaped this

v1 followed the v4 style bible's "no humans, artifacts or buildings" clause and came back flat; the founder rejected the look. The rebuild is fully populated cinematic reconstruction — people as silhouettes, hands, backs; props; architecture; volumetric light; shallow depth of field; grain. Six worlds carry the film: A01 deep water descent, A02 seabed survey, A03 the sealing world, A04 clay & impressions, A05 museum study, A06 Alexandria. Every edit was generated from its approved anchor as the reference image, so grade, palette and light direction carry across each family.
