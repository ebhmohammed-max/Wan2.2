# Style v2 — cinematic rebuild (founder decision, 2026-11-04)

## What changed and why

v1 followed `11_Style_and_Continuity_Bible_v4` literally: six **anonymous material/background families**, explicitly "no artifacts, ruins, divers", "no humans, artifacts or buildings". The founder reviewed the delivered plates against his own earlier Trojan War work and rejected the look as flat: not 3D-cinematic, no characters, no objects. Cause was compliance with the v4 spec, not generation failure.

**Decision (founder, explicit):** rebuild all 30 plates as populated cinematic scenes — people, objects, architecture, volumetric light. Style reference supplied: his `Trojan_War` folders
(`10a-uGdIdMdMhvHl0q5_VpRl0PGEFkhp6`), e.g. `TROY_001_Troad_Inspired_Dawn_Landscape`, `TROY_003_Inhabited_Courtyard_Daily_Life`.

## What survives from v4 — the part that keeps the film honest

These images are **illustrative reconstruction**, not evidence. Standing rules:

1. The **actual ring, its lighthouse engraving, the British Museum coin and the Met bulla remain real photographs** in the evidence panels. No generated frame substitutes for a documentary object image, and no generated coin/ring may be captioned as the find.
2. Every rebuilt plate carries an editor label: `Illustrative reconstruction — not the excavation`.
3. No legible inscription, lettering or numeral is generated anywhere. Ring faces and coin faces are turned away or unreadable in frame.
4. Faces stay anonymous — divers masked, workers as silhouettes, hands cropped at the wrist. No invented portrait of an administrator or a merchant.
5. The sourced Met bulla INSERT (C02-S005-A) is still acquired, never generated.
