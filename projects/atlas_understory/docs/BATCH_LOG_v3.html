The one decision that shaped this

v1 followed the v4 style bible's "no humans, artifacts or buildings" clause and came back flat; the founder rejected the look. The rebuild is fully populated cinematic reconstruction — people as silhouettes, hands, backs; props; architecture; volumetric light; shallow depth of field; grain. Six worlds carry the film: A01 deep water descent, A02 seabed survey, A03 the sealing world, A04 clay & impressions, A05 museum study, A06 Alexandria. Every edit was generated from its approved anchor as the reference image, so grade, palette and light direction carry across each family.

The extra imagery pass — 19 new plates for the back half

Founder instruction after the audit: the script, shot order, durations and overlays stay exactly as the v4 handoff — create images as much as is needed. The audit (Drive Doc 02 — Shot-map audit, 1OXAYFeKUI-UVVEZ7Z46ZjA7xr_NiXW-a_sFCYQdRr-8) found one real weakness in the delivered film: 19 of the 49 plate positions were marked REUSE, so from Chapter 4 onward the audience was being shown crops of images it had already seen twice.

Each slot was given a genuinely new moment inside the same world, at the same framing the shot map asks for — close on the odd plates, oblique medium on the even ones — with the same motion recipe and the same duration. Nothing in the timeline moved.

How a repeat was caught before it shipped

Editing a reference image can return a near-copy of it. Rather than eyeballing 19 results, every new plate was measured against its anchor: mean absolute difference over a 192×108 greyscale reduction, 22 or below treated as a clone. Nine of the first pass scored 31–55 and passed; two failed and were re-shot — C04-S006 (6.86 → 49.7) and C04-S012 (2.01 → 72.4) — and one, C04-S002, was also rejected by eye because a carved-looking relief had appeared on a stone that is meant to be blank and eroded. It was regenerated as bare limestone with no carving. This test is why the pass took two rounds rather than one.
