Status 2026-11-04: acted on and closed. Tier 1 was taken — all 19 REUSE plate positions now have their own image, delivered and cut. Tier 2 was decided and left as scripted (see the note under Table C). Tier 3 remains optional. The analysis below is kept as written, because it is the reasoning the delivery rests on.

Read of the whole v4 Shot Map (98 shots, frames 0–15,745 @ 24 fps) against what is actually delivered. The script, the shot order, the durations and the overlays are treated as frozen. This audit only asks one question: at which positions could a generated image still be added without moving a single frame of the timeline?

The map at a glance

  • 98 shots. 49 background-plate shots, 46 editor-graphic shots (42 DOC/ARTICLE, 3 DATA VIZ, 1 MAP), 3 real-source-photo shots.
  • 30 generated plates in the map's own budget (6 anchors + 24 edits) — all delivered, mastered 3840×2160, each with a clip.
  • 19 plate positions were marked REUSE — Chapter 4 replayed Chapter 1's six images, Chapter 5 replayed Chapter 2's. Zero new images were budgeted; 19 were generated anyway and delivered, so no plate repeats in the film.
  • 1 sourced photograph (C02-S005-A) plus 2 further photo shots that reuse it (C02-S007, C02-S009).
  • 46 shots carry no imagery of mine at all — they are editor graphics, type panels and diagrams.

How Tier 1 was executed

Each of the 19 slots was given a new moment inside its own world, at the framing this table assigns — close on the odd plates, oblique medium on the even ones — generated from the family anchor so grade and light carry, then mastered and cut at the shot map's own duration and recipe. Two safeguards:

  • Clone test. Every new plate was compared numerically to its anchor (mean absolute difference at 192×108, greyscale); 22 or below counts as a near-copy and is re-shot. First pass: two failures, C04-S006 (6.86 → 49.7) and C04-S012 (2.01 → 72.4).
  • Eye check. C04-S002 passed the numeric test but showed a carved-looking relief on a stone that must read as blank and eroded, so it was regenerated as bare limestone with no carving — a reminder that the measurement catches clones, not plausibility.

Result: 49 plate positions, 49 distinct images, 49 clips, the same 327.71 s of timeline. Details and file ids are in 01 — Batch Log and 03 — Delivery index.

Recommendation (as originally written)

Take Tier 1: 19 new plates for the reuse slots, in the shot map's own framing assignments, generated from each family's approved anchor so grade and light still carry. The film keeps every second it has; the second half simply starts showing the viewer something it has not already shown them. Tier 1 also quietly solves a continuity risk — a plate that appears four times across 23 minutes is the first thing an attentive viewer notices.

Everything here is additive. The 49 existing clips stay valid until replacements are approved, so there is never a moment where the film is missing a plate.
