## How each was screened
- Numeric clone test against its anchor, as above.
- Eye check at full size for the three things the numbers cannot catch: baked lettering, glyph-like marks that read as
  inscription, and recognisable faces.
- **T3-A05 was re-shot once.** The first version had stamped impressions on the sealings that read as glyph-like designs at full
  size, which crosses the standing no-inscription rule. The delivered version has worn, indistinct surfaces and no discernible
  design on any object.

## Standing rules that were held
No legible inscription, legend or numeral anywhere; ring and coin faces blank or turned away; anonymous figures only — backs,
silhouettes and out-of-focus faces; no baked text, labels or watermarks; no VO, music, titles or captions. Architecture plain.
Every plate mastered the same way as the film: 16:9 crop → Lanczos 3840×2160 → unsharp 2.0/55 %/thr 3 → grain σ2.2 → −6 % vignette.
