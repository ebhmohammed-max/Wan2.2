**Batch:** 046 of 105 · `15:00–15:20` · 10 frames · 2 seconds/frame · Ch. IV

**Visual standard:** Photoreal live-action 65mm historical cinema, ARRI Alexa 65, Panavision Primo spherical primes, Kodak Vision3 500T, physically grounded detail, restrained natural film grain.

**Continuity:** *If you consult the dark annals of Asshai-by-the-Shadow on how humble shepherds became dragonriders, you will hear a tale that strips Valyria of its proudest boast. In those bleak, candle-lit libraries beside the black River Ash, ancient scrolls insist that the Valyrians did…*

**The film cuts away from Valyria to its rival account.** Asshai does not merely disagree with the Valyrian version — it *strips Valyria of its proudest boast*, which is the claim that dragonriding was a Valyrian achievement. This is the first time in the episode that a second source speaks, and the look has to carry that: black stone that eats light, red lanterns, and an archive that is hoarding rather than sharing.

**Two collisions had to be solved before a single frame was rendered, and both were solved by substitution rather than compromise.**

1. *"Candle-lit libraries"* against `LOC-ESS-003`, which is locked to **dim blood-red shadowbinder lanterns** under perpetual twilight. Rendering warm candle-gold here would have broken Asshai the way gold broke Qarth. **Solution:** the candle is carried by **one or two guttering tallow flames** — weak, yellow-grey, smoking more than burning — while the room's colour stays blood-red. `0457` makes the substitution literal by putting the dying candle in the same frame as the red.
2. *"Ancient scrolls insist"* against the production's hardest ban: **nothing in the frame may read as writing, script, glyph, rune, numeral, tally, map or diagram.** A library whose subject is written records, in a production that cannot show writing. **Solution:** the precedent from `IMG0374` and `IMG0284` — **solve it by absence.** Every scroll in this batch is **rolled, closed, cord-bound, wax-sealed, caged or turned end-out**, and the archive's authority comes from *quantity, weight and dust* instead of legibility. It reads as abundance without text, which is exactly what a forbidden library looks like.

**`IMG0451` is the strongest frame of the batch and the cheapest lesson in it.** Hundreds of scroll cylinders stacked in niches of light-drinking black stone, one blood-red lantern on an iron bracket and one tallow flame at the floor, both giving more shadow than light. No text, no open page, no diagram — and the frame still says *this place knows more than you do*, because quantity and darkness carry authority that legibility never could. **A library shot for a production that cannot show words has to be built out of weight.**

**`IMG0453` failed on architecture, and the failure is instructive because it is the caldera lesson arriving somewhere new.** At 2x the gallery has **moulded capitals and bases** on the piers, **regular courses of dressed ashlar** with visible joints, and a **coffered floor of square pavers with grout lines** — Roman basilica, in other words, not cyclopean Asshai. The location bible is explicit: Asshai's stone is monolithic, sharp-angled, **jointless**, and light-drinking. Dressed masonry with a capital is the single most reliable way a model signals "classical building", and this production has now been caught by it twice — once in the caldera at `IMG0286`, where the columns rendered as fortress rampart, and here as a temple. **The countermeasure that worked there is the same one: say what must NOT be there, by name.** The re-render brief negates *ashlar, dressed stone, coursed masonry, square pavers, column capital, base moulding, cornice, entablature*, and requires the floor to be one continuous worn pour.

There is a second, subtler problem in `0453`: the hoods read as **flat dark masks on upright bodies**, which is what makes the figure line look like a modern reading room rather than a processional of shadowed figures. The fix is in the brief — **hoods hanging lower than the crown of the head, figures bent forward and heavy-shouldered**, so the shape of a person disappears into cloth.

**Lessons to carry into 047:**

1. **A collision in the brief is a design decision, not a blocker.** *Candle-lit* against *blood-red lanterns* did not need a compromise look — it needed one guttering tallow flame per frame and no warmth anywhere else. Say which half of the conflict you are honouring and let the other become a detail.
2. **Bans are best satisfied by absence.** Ten frames about scrolls, hundreds of scrolls, zero legible marks. The `0374`/`0284` precedent held again because it was made a rule instead of a preference.
3. **Classical architecture creeps in wherever a pier is described.** Any column, any capital, any paver will be rendered as Roman unless negated by name. This is the architecture equivalent of the extra-limb rule: **specify the negative, not just the positive.**
4. **Depth of field did the heavy lifting this batch.** `0457`'s long lens turning hundreds of scrolls into red murk said more about the archive's scale than any wide shot could, at a fraction of the cost.
5. **Anonymous figures work best when the hood hangs below the skull line.** An upright head under a hood gives the model a face-plane to fill; a bowed one gives it only cloth.
6. **Ship the strongest frame first.** `0451` was generation one and became the batch's anchor; the rest were matched to it rather than the reverse.
