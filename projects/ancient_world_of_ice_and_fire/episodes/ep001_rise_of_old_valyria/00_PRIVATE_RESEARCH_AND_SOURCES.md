## 1. SOURCE HIERARCHY & CANON RULES APPLIED

All historical claims in Episode 001 strictly adhere to the following published canon hierarchy:
1. **Primary Historical Compendium:** *The World of Ice & Fire: The Untold History of Westeros and the Game of Thrones* (George R. R. Martin, Elio M. García Jr., and Linda Antonsson, 2014) — abbreviated **TWOIAF**.
2. **Dynastic & Dragon Lore History:** *Fire & Blood* (George R. R. Martin, 2018) — abbreviated **F&B**.
3. **Core Novels (*A Song of Ice and Fire*):**
   - *A Game of Thrones* (1996) — **AGOT**
   - *A Clash of Kings* (1998) — **ACOK**
   - *A Storm of Swords* (2000) — **ASOS**
   - *A Feast for Crows* (2005) — **AFFC**
   - *A Dance with Dragons* (2011) — **ADWD**
4. **Cartographic Reference:** *The Lands of Ice and Fire* (George R. R. Martin, 2012) — **TLOIAF**.
5. **Supplementary Novellas:** *A Knight of the Seven Kingdoms* — **AKOTSK**.

### Television vs. Book Canon Divergences Enforced
- **Dragon Anatomy:** Strictly **four limbs** (two muscular hind legs + two bat-like forelimb wings). Hexapod dragons (four legs + two wings) are strictly prohibited. (*AGOT*, *F&B*, GRRM authorial canon).
- **Valyrian Eye Color:** Explicitly **purple, violet, lilac, indigo, or deep amethyst** (omitted in HBO's *Game of Thrones*, restored here per *AGOT* Daenerys I & *TWOIAF*).
- **Ghiscari Harpy Iconography:** In **Old Ghis**, the Harpy clutched a **thunderbolt** between her talons—NOT chains (which belonged specifically to Astapor's later derivative harpy) and NOT a whip/collar (Yunkai). (*ASOS* Daenerys III).
- **Ghiscari Dress & Hair:** Freeborn Ghiscari nobility wore the fringed **`tokar`** (wound around the body and draped over the left arm, requiring the wearer to hold it in place—a deliberate mark of leisure proving the wearer did not perform manual labor) and oiled/wired their dark-and-reddish hair into **horns, wings, and blades**. (*ASOS* Daenerys III; *ADWD* Daenerys I–II).
- **Chronology & Anachronism Guardrails:**
  - **No invented Valyrian names** are attributed to 5,000 BC historical actors.
  - **House Targaryen** is referenced *only* as forward-looking historical context (one of the forty dragonlord houses of the later Freehold, far from the mightiest); Aenar Targaryen (~126 BC) is NOT placed in early Valyria.
  - **Named dragons** (Balerion, Meraxes, Vhagar) belong millennia later and do NOT appear in Episode 001.
  - **Valyrian Steel** is treated as an advanced spell-forged metallurgical achievement developed *after* Valyria mastered dragonfire and pyromancy—early pastoral Valyrians worked stone, bone, wool, leather, and bronze/copper before rising to spell-forged steel.

## 3. CONTINUITY LOCK FOR SUBSEQUENT EPISODES
- **Episode 002 Handoff:** Episode 001 must end *before* the outbreak of the First Ghiscari War, stopping at the exact geopolitical threshold where young Valyria's dragonlords and slave-mines draw the hostile envy of Old Ghis across the Gulf of Grief.
- **No Premature Reveals:** Do not narrate the destruction of Old Ghis (save as a brief thematic foreshadow in the opening hook), the Rhoynish Wars, the founding of the Free Cities, Braavos/the Faceless Men's founding rebellion (reserve the Kindly Man's slave revolt & first Faceless Man for the dedicated Freehold/Braavos episode!), or the Doom of Valyria. In Episode 001, mention *only* the beginning of deep volcanic mining and human bondage beneath the Fourteen Flames so later episodes have full dramatic room to tell the origin of Braavos and the Faceless Men!
