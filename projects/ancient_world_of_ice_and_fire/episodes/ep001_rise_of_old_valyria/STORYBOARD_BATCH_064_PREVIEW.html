Stage C arrives — crimson, spun gold, scale; no crown

This batch is the narration's first crimson passage (“tall, silver-haired, draped now in crimson wool, spun gold, and overlapping bronze-and-iron scale”). Register held on the far side of the line: the vestments appear; the crown never does — the “sacred inheritance” clause lands in Batch 065 and is deliberately absent here. Gold, ruby, bronze and scales take light; nothing glows; the lava canals of the Freehold are rendered DEAD (crusted, unlit, no water); the only pale value in the batch is light on water, frame-east.

The 0638 three-pass arc — two failure classes in one frame

0638 v1 invited a castle: crenellated parapet, rectangular slab-grid perch, a possible specular eye-glint. v2 fixed exactly those and then regressed the identity marker — blond, curly hair, no braids (Stage C = luminous silver-gold warrior braid). v3 holds both: organic jagged fused-stone parapets, cooled-flow slab floor of irregular polygons, the dragon coiled with its head tucked away — no eye, no glint, limbs not enumerable (annotated). Twice rejected; the record says so.

Two canon locks tested and held

No sun, no golden hour. v2 of 0639 arrived as peach-orange sunset — re-rendered flat iron-grey; v1 of 0640 arrived with a blown-out sun bloom — re-rendered with zero sun anywhere. The peninsula stays pewter until the story itself warms. Nothing is worked stone. 0636 v1 returned classical drum columns with capitals and near-black hair — re-rendered to irregular poured hex-basalt and silver-gold braids; 0634 v1 returned reflective water pools in a rectangular grid — re-rendered to dry dead channels and organic jagged hex-basalt. The fused-stone rule bent twice and held both times.
