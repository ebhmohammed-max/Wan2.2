**Structure — one location, ten frames, and a birth.** Every frame in this batch is inside `LOC-VAL-003`, the high caldera. It moves from the adult animal at full power (`0381` walking, `0382` asleep, `0383` a sleeper in its lair) through the eggs as objects (`0384`, `0386`) and the nesting basin at scale (`0385`) to the hatching itself (`0387`–`0390`). The sequence escalates by **scale of the subject**, not by amount of detail — which is also why it was the cleanest batch so far.

**Standing directives carried forward, plus three new ones:**
- **The five-part silhouette signature remains mandatory.** Deep barrel chest, thick short ringed neck, heavy blunt wedge skull with broad crocodilian snout and two swept-back horns, wing-arms folded tight, long thick tapering tail.
- **Grounded dragons carry their weight on the hind legs.** `0381` establishes the walking stance: two taloned hind legs planted, wings held half-spread as counterbalance only, thick tail out behind. The wings must never look like extra legs.
- **Smoke is dark grey, never white.** Thin semi-transparent filaments from fissures and shell seams; white steam, bright fog and pale crystal columns are all negated in every frame.
- **NEW — the egg is a hard textured object.** Scale-textured with fine overlapping ridges, matte sheen, ashy mottling, roughly torso-sized. Never smooth, never glossy, never pale and speckled like a bird's egg.
- **NEW — a newborn animal cannot be photographed whole.** `0388` pass 1 came back a glossy figurine. A hatchling described in full reads as a manufactured object; it must be shot as a **portrait** — head, thick neck and one shoulder, wet matte scales, the shell rim cutting the frame.
- **NEW — heat is implied, not displayed.** A dull ember seam in a fissure, a faint glow deep inside a split shell. No fire masses, no lava, no fountains.

**Rerender brief used (resolved):**

1. **`IMG0388` — the figurine.** Pass 1 produced a glossy resin-black hatchling with a spiky crest, a slender curved neck and a collectible-toy stance: a whole animal described in isolation defaults to a manufactured object. Fixed by changing the *shot*, not just the words — tight 85mm profile of head, thick short neck and one shoulder only, the rest of the body out of frame, with **wet matte** scales, a **heavy blunt wedge** skull and no crest, plus explicit negatives for toy, figurine, doll, plastic model and glossy resin. The shell rim cuts diagonally across the lower left to place it in the world.

**Lessons from this batch to carry into 040:**

1. **A newborn creature must be shot as a portrait, never as a whole body.** Full-body description of an unfamiliar animal at small scale is exactly the prompt shape that produces toys. Fragment the subject instead: head, neck, shoulder, one folded wing, one claw.
2. **A hatching sequence escalates by scale, not by detail.** Egg as object → egg against an endless wall → claw through the seam → portrait of the newborn → folded wing → the animal alone and tiny against the landscape. Six frames, no repeated idea, and no frame needing much descriptive text.
3. **Grounded stance is a distinct problem from flying stance.** Weight on two taloned hind legs, wings used purely as counterbalance, thick tail out behind. Without that the walking animal becomes a biped ape or a wyvern.
4. **Heat reads best as a single dull seam.** Every warm element in this batch is one contained line of ember in rock or shell; nothing glowed broadly and nothing read as fire.
5. **`0381` is the new reference frame for the animal.** When a dragon pose is uncertain, match the walking animal: plated barrel chest, thick ringed neck, blunt horned wedge skull, wing-arms folded or half-spread for balance, thick tapering tail.
6. **Location depth is free scale.** The same basalt wall, the same ash flats and the same grey haze appear in eight frames, and each one reads as a different place because the camera height and distance change radically — inside a hollow, on a ledge, at the lens, from the rim.
