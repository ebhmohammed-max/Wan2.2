The foreshadow delivered as scale, object and absence

The batch renders the “house named Targaryen — far from the mightiest” as PLACEMENT, OBJECT and ABSENCE, never as prophecy. 0741 opens under the self-ruling city: HIS empty hands, the EX-ARCHON ordinary elder passing him with a single nod, the FLATSTONE unused — the law fully institutionalized. 0742 His face doing arithmetic: the office denied, the gate to monarchy shut. 0743 the close of assembly day: one elder carries his own tally-kit back to the common rack — the office returns to the many without ceremony. 0744 deep time as EXPOSED MARINE STRATA cut by generations of buried footpaths — millennia compressed into worn stone. 0745 the whole peninsula from above: exactly the crescent scatter of the forty, all equally low, all equally unlit, all smoke WEST — the scene a check on any impulse to rank. 0746 the frame everyone will remember: the freestanding legs end in open sky; on the EASTERN fringe, one horizon-edge DOT with one thin WEST ribbon of smoke. 0747 the minor mark at macro: one smooth low upward chevron with short flat sweep-wing arcs — peace. 0748 HIS face weighing hierarchy over a bare tally-ledge — the intimacy of rank. 0749 the thesis in one image: two bare hands pinning a BLANK roll of dark linen, ONE tiny carbon forked-wing stamp at the centre — the hugeness of the blank next to the smallest seal — the Targaryens confined to measured absence. 0750 the crescent stepping down toward the sea: the mightiest held against the cones at WEST; the far eastern holds hang lightest against the chalk sea.
