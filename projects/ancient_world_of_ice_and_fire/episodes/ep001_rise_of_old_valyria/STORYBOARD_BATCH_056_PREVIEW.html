The rule that governed this batch

Barth's sentence, not Barth's fantasy. Septon Barth never saw Valyria; the narration gives us his categories, and the images render the shape of a claim made at a distance — never the act. Three devices in rotation: residue instead of event (0551), the category as a specimen (0552–0554), and the boundary rather than the act (0555–0559). 0560 turns the batch back to the reader. Nothing in the ten frames depicts mating, altering, restraint or any creature alive with a person; the only dragons are four dead shapes at a distance in 0555 — geology, not animals. The hard bans (no cage, pen, harness, collar, chain, restraint, blood, wound, ritual circle) are honoured throughout.
