**The sceptic concedes, and the concession is carried by scale.** This is the hinge of the chapter's argument: Yandel has spent four minutes dismantling the eastern claim and now grants it one point he cannot argue away. **The frames do not make the argument — the narration does.** What the frames supply is the **evidence** the argument rests on: a structure no shepherd built, and a material that can only have been melted.

**`IMG0491` is the first architecture Valyria is allowed to have that dwarfs a person without ornamenting itself.** A 24mm from the foot of the spire, tilted steeply up so the shaft leaves the frame at the top and its two fused spurs leave it at different heights — a hard black vertical against flat grey cloud, leaning and twisting a few degrees as it climbs, **no joint line, no course, no mortar, no capital, no base.** Three robed figures stand at the base, tiny, faceless. **Vertigo, and no mason in sight** — which is the point: the structure is not evidence of skill, it is evidence of something that was not built at all.

**`IMG0492` is the whole Valyrian claim in a piece of surface.** An 85mm macro filled entirely by cooled fused stone: the smooth rippled skin of something that was liquid and hardened, **flow lines running through it like the surface of poured glass**, fine grey ash settled in the ripples, small vesicles, one hairline cooling crack — and in the centre, where the stone has fractured, a fresh break showing a **faint green-black translucency a few millimetres into the thin edge.**

**That translucency is the argument.** It is the one visual detail in the production that cannot be produced by stacking, coursing, mortaring or dressing stone. You can build a tower out of blocks; you cannot make the edge of a block go translucent the way thick obsidian does. **The material is the proof, and a macro is the only lens that delivers it.**

**Notes for `IMG0493`–`IMG0500`, the frames still to render:**

- **`0493`–`0495` finish the Citadel's argument on the sceptic's side** before the trials open: a close detail, a tactile light study, and a wide horizon that bridges back out of the institution.
- **`0496` opens the cavern trials** with a 28mm low-angle monumental frame. **The fire, glyph, sound and blood trials are the most dangerous material in the episode** and must be shot as **physical and procedural** — heat, soot, scored stone, breath, torchlight on wet rock — never as magic, never as glowing runes, never as a light show. No incandescent glyphs, no energy, no aura, no levitation.
- **Every living face in the trials stays out of the frame or in silhouette.** Seven batches of precedent now back the rule that this production is stronger when faces are withheld.
- **Negate by name and every time:** no floating symbols, no glowing script, no magic circles, no energy trails, no lens flares, no god-light, no levitation, no glowing eyes.

**The sceptic's case closed on three objects.** `0493` puts a fist-sized shard of fused stone on a desk in candlelight with one ink-stained fingertip entering frame — **a whole empire reduced to something a man could put in his pocket.** `0494` takes the same shard to 100mm on a cracked leather cover: flow ripples, vesicles, dust in the hollows, a thread of spider silk, and the **translucent green-black break** that no worked stone can produce. `0495` leaves the room for a low black wall alone on a grey shore in sea mist, its **relief carving worn past legibility** — silent riddles, a message with the message gone.

**The cavern produced both of this batch's rejections, and both were caught only at magnification.** The first `0496` returned **glistening wet folded rock that read as viscera** — an anatomical interior rather than stone. `0498` returned a soft rounded form in the heat haze legible as **a foot with toes coming apart**. Neither was visible at 950px; both were obvious at 5x and 7x.

**`0496` passed on the second pass** as dry, matte, dust-grey and charcoal basalt: sharp angular fractured slabs, ash on every ledge, hard chipped edges, deep cracks, no sheen anywhere, and the vault above twenty feet **pure black with no opening and no glow.** One coal trench running to a vanishing point, three faceless figures at its near end.

**The generalisable correction: the trials must be shot drier, darker and more broken than instinct wants.** A smooth dark wet surface under warm light is the exact recipe that produces viscera, and the model will use it every time it is offered.

**`0500` was rejected on character continuity rather than quality.** In isolation it is a strong frame — a bowed figure at a trench, cupped soot-black trembling hands, coals blooming behind — but it delivered **a grey-stubbled man of forty with livid red streaking down his back** where the sequence has established **youths with short ash-stiffened hair**, and the red reads as blood in a production that has shown none. That would hand the trials to a character we have not met.

**Lessons to carry into 051:**

1. **Darkness is the anti-magic tool.** Push the cavern toward black and the incandescent, the symbolic and the anatomical all lose their footing.
2. **Gloss is viscera.** Any prompt that pairs a wet smooth surface with warm light will get membrane and organ. Matte and angular, every time.
3. **Check the character, not just the frame.** `0500` would have passed an image-only review and failed the sequence.
4. **A still object beats a speech.** Three frames of a shard and a wall say what the narration takes a paragraph to argue.
5. **Blur, erosion and illegibility are allies.** `0495`'s worn carving satisfies the "riddles" beat without a single legible glyph, exactly as the Citadel pages did.
