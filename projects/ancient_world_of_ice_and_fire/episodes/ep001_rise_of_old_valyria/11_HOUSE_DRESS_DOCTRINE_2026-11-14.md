## 1. THE FIXED INVARIANTS (the forty are ONE PEOPLE) — NEVER VIOLATED

Every Valyrian, of every house, carries ALL of the following, always:

- Hair: platinum / silver-white / pale silver-gold. NEVER brown, black, red, auburn, chestnut. Foaled ancestry shows as *shade* (champagne-silver vs frost) at most — never hue.
- Eyes: violet / amethyst / lilac (anything in that band; never brown, never cartoon-bright).
- Face: narrow mask-bone alien beauty — high cheekbones, almond eyes, long jaw. The race apart.
- Skin read: pale-marble to weathered sea-glass; never earth-brown (that is for foreigners — and the CONTRAST IS THE HORROR).
- Comportment: languid dragonlord hauteur; they do not hurry; they do not emote broadly; they do not sweat.
- Metal: obsidian / black glass / tarnished gold / dulled bronze / unmarked torcs. NO crowns. NO jewels that hold light like a jewel. GOLD-DULL and SORCERY-FLAT absolute.

**Houses differ ONLY in:** gown palette (muted, desaturated — never saturated primaries) · weave/material · accessory metal and form · grooming custom (falls, plait, rope, veil) · manner/custom. The fixed invariants stay untouched.

## 2. THE TWELVE SIGNATURE LINES (the Forty Houses wearing twelve dressed lines — roughly three-to-four minor houses echo each line; the twelve below ARE the visible grammar)

**Line 01 — HOUSE VELARYON** *(the one canon-anchored line — seacoast dragonlords; forward-looking lore anchor)*
- Palette: deep slate blue-grey + whispers of dull silver.
- Material: storm-silk with a wave-sheen weave (matte, never glossy).
- Accessory: hammered SILVER torc, open ends; unworked dull pearl (never lustrous) set at the throat.
- Grooming: falls coiled low at the nape under a salt-cord; loose strands always salt-white.
- Manner: listens longer than all; speaks last; still hands on the table.
- Colour cue on camera: blue-grey at the collar amid black-and-ash rooms.

**Line 02 — HOUSE OLYX** *(the forge yards; the loud smiths-of-fire elevated to dragonlords)*
- Palette: dark bronze-black + dried rust accent (muted).
- Material: hammered scale-lamellar silk; soot-warp reverse side.
- Accessory: blunt BRONZE torc, ends capped flat as anvils; one dark amber-dull forge-glass bead on a thong.
- Grooming: half the falls bound hard against one temple with a wire ring.
- Manner: direct, clipped; argues like striking metal; no wasted syllable.
- Colour cue: bronze glints at the shoulder in gloom.

**Line 03 — HOUSE MYRAND** *(the mineral wealth; the digging cousins)*
- Palette: ash-grey + leaf-flat dull gold.
- Material: leaf-shot matte charmeuse, heavier drape.
- Accessory: tarnished gold leaf-WIRE chain across the chest — never a torc; dust-caked boots worn proud.
- Grooming: falls loose to the waist, ends mineral-dusted (literal, dull, never glitter).
- Manner: acquisitive courtesy; prices everything, including people.
- Colour cue: the single dull gold note in a black hall.

**Line 04 — HOUSE SYTHIS** *(the northern ridge line; cold pride)*
- Palette: bone-white + ash grey.
- Material: frost-moor wool threaded with silver filament.
- Accessory: cold SILVER torc worn flat as a band, no ornament.
- Grooming: falls plaited into one heavy triple plait at the nape, silver wire inside.
- Manner: slow, exact; courtesy like a blade laid on black cloth; never raises voice.
- Colour cue: the pale band of white in a black room.

**Line 05 — HOUSE CORVAN** *(the roost-keepers; the wyvern-tenders)*
- Palette: raven-black + matte obsidian.
- Material: feather-light black velvet-silk; never actual wings on cloth (no costume-feathers).
- Accessory: one unpolished OBSIDIAN drop pendant, palm-sized, worn on the brow-cord.
- Grooming: falls swept forward over ONE shoulder, always.
- Manner: sudden stillness; watches wings and doors, never faces.
- Colour cue: a single forward black fall crossing a black robe.

**Line 06 — HOUSE TESSEK** *(the rope-ways; the mine-side houses made high)*
- Palette: raw umber + tar-dark brown.
- Material: cord-weave panels (tarred fibre laid into weave) over silk body.
- Accessory: ONE LOOP of genuine haul-rope fused in bronze at the wrist — the only tactile joke among the proud.
- Grooming: the eldest wears one rope-thick plait; every other falls cropped at the jaw.
- Manner: counts silently; never sits first; stands when others are seated.
- Colour cue: tar-brown texture where all others wear black.

**Line 07 — HOUSE VHAENYS** *(the old volcanic court; the thrice-burned)*
- Palette: charcoal + deep dim crimson thread (muted brick, never bright).
- Material: slag-silk — volcanic-glass filament woven matte; catches NO light.
- Accessory: flat black-glass torc; NO other metal anywhere on the body.
- Grooming: falls veiled BACK from the forehead, always, eyes bare.
- Manner: speaks only in verdicts; one sentence, then silence.
- Colour cue: the single brick-red thread read at the cuff, that's all.

**Line 08 — HOUSE OSSAR** *(the sphinx-keepers; the terrace line)*
- Palette: chalk-grey + root-brown.
- Material: lime-bleached matte linen with root-fibre overweave.
- Accessory: strings of fused chalk-glass beads, dull white.
- Grooming: falls wrapped at the brow with a chalk cord, showing only the mask-face.
- Manner: asks questions, never answers one; collects riddles, not tributes.
- Colour cue: the chalk-white cord over the brow.

**Line 09 — HOUSE ILVAREN** *(the cloud line; the high terraces on the wind)*
- Palette: cloud-grey + pearl-dull (never lustrous).
- Material: layered half-sheer cloud-spun silk; wind-catching width.
- Accessory: pale grey pearl torc — one strand, dull.
- Grooming: falls loose and ALWAYS wind-swept in motion; volume over order.
- Manner: arrives late, speaks first; the room must turn.
- Colour cue: the one figure the wind is always dressing.

**Line 10 — HOUSE DREVAN** *(the dusk line; the old archon blood — ritual courtesy absolute)*
- Palette: deep indigo-grey + dim violet tinge (muted, never royal).
- Material: dusk-weave with silver rite-threads at the hems only.
- Accessory: ONE amethyst-drop earring (the only ear ornament in the hall).
- Grooming: falls bound at BOTH temples with two narrow silver cords.
- Manner: ritual courtesy; never raises voice; the boast delivered as liturgy.
- Colour cue: the paired temple cords + the single drop at the ear.

**Line 11 — HOUSE TARYS** *(the new-made; the rope-wealth climbers — deliberately uncouth by dragonlord code)*
- Palette: black + ONE thread of red-copper glint at the hem.
- Material: black-dyed leather-silk laminate.
- Accessory: red-copper torc, freshly forged (dulled but unmistakably new).
- Grooming: falls chopped straight at the jaw — the one line that treats the falls as a nuisance.
- Manner: laughs at the old houses; blunt; the toast nobody asked for.
- Colour cue: the single red-copper thread + squared jaw-length hair.

**Line 12 — THE ROCK-BOUND LINE** *(reference-only: the forward-looking Targaryen ancestor-class — intentionally the LEAST grand of the twelve — per the production bible, "far from the mightiest")*
- Palette: flat soot-brown + ash.
- Material: hardy undyed dragon-leather and rough silk.
- Accessory: simple black leather wrist-cord; no torcs, no gold, no glass.
- Grooming: falls loose, sun-dried, slightly ragged — untamed, wind-roughened.
- Manner: speaks little at the table; watches the sky past the roosts.
- Colour cue: the one figure underdressed at the summit.

## 3. APPLYING THE LAW ON CAMERA

- **Background crowds** (convocations, assemblies, markets of the high): dress 3–5 DIFFERENT lines visible at once; the room must read as plural houses, never as one obsidian uniform.
- **Named individuals** (orators, archons, lords): the line must be legible from silhouette + one colour note; never a costume-plate inventory.
- **Enslaved / foreigners remain earth-toned** — the contrast law is untouched.
- **Never confuse with "costuming for the show"**: Valyrians never theme a look per event; the dress IS the house, always.
- **No two lines share a palette or a metal** — if you cannot tell them apart at a glance on a thumbnail, the prompt is wrong.
- **DRG-VAL-002 unaffected; GOLD-DULL / SORCERY-FLAT / NO-TEXT / PEWTER / SLATE-SEA-FAR-RIGHT / WEST-dormant-volcano untouched.**
