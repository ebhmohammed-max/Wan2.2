**`0503` is the batch in one image, and it was the hardest to get.** Two soot-black pairs of hands winding a strip of torn grey linen around a dusty forearm, cropped at the collarbone so no head is ever in frame. **An empire's founding technology turns out to be a bandage** — that is the exact register the narration is asking for when it says *centuries of lethal trial and error among the calderas*, and it is the sort of image that reads as documentary rather than as myth.

**It also cost a generation to moderation.** The original brief described a forearm *blistered and reddened along one edge*, and the request was refused outright. Rebuilt around **soot, sweat, dust and linen** with no injury language at all, it passed first time and produced a better frame. **The narration says "blood"; the images must never depict it. Describe the residue, not the wound.** That rule now governs the rest of the episode.

**`0504` is the coldest image in the film so far, and it is not cruel.** Eleven exhausted candidates seated against a low rock shelf; four dark shapes lying flat under coarse strips of cloth out on the floor; **nobody kneeling over them, nobody looking at them.** The frame refuses to mourn and refuses to dramatise. It just does the arithmetic.

**`0506` is how the episode handles "something unprecedented" without cheating.** A great natural fissure in dry black basalt — jagged, spalled, ash in the cracks — with **heat glowing dull red inside the rock** and fading to black as the crack climbs. No flames. No glow spilling out. At the foot, a single child-sized figure with their head tilted back. **It is only heat in a crack in the ground**, and that is precisely right for a chapter that has spent sixteen minutes refusing magic.

**Notes for `IMG0507`–`0510`:**

- **`0507` and `0508` carry the "glyph" beat**, and the writing ban applies in full: **no legible symbol, character, rune or numeral anywhere.** The glyph must be **scratched or scored into stone, worn, partial and unreadable** — a mark someone made, not a sign that glows. Precedent: `0495`'s eroded wall, the Citadel's blurred pages.
- **`0509` renders "blood" as material, not as injury** — a dark stain soaked into cloth or ash, dried, matte, unremarkable. **No wound, no wound-bed, no body, no fresh flow.**
- **`0510` bridges out** on a 40mm wide, and should end the trials on distance rather than on drama.
- **Every negation from §4e still applies**: no magic, no glowing runes, no energy, no aura, no sparks, no god-light, no gloss or wet sheen, no membrane, no flesh, no levitation, no dragon, no face.
