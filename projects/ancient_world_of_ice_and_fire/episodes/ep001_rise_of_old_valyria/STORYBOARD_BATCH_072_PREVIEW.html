The arc of the commons — negation by absence

0711 opens inside the press — the assembly is not a ceremony, it is a room you can walk through. 0712 renders the law as a HOLE: a bare stone where a throne would sit, acknowledged by being left vacant. 0713 has the speaker hold the floor at ground level — no elevation of office exists to climb. 0714 is the gathering at its largest — and it is COLD: not one fracture in frame burns. 0715 shows what the law is built on — many small smoking hands of land, no hand owning all. 0716 holds the two peaks above the rim like two unanswerable parents. 0717 literally shows the vote. 0718 rests on one listening face, a face that could belong to anyone. 0719 shows what the law actually is — a stone worn smooth by repetition, not carved by decree. 0720 begins to loosen toward evening, and on the far western ridge one smoke hangs thicker than the rest — the forty, seeded, not yet a subject.

What failed, honestly

0714 v1 gave the western ridge vent a visible warm orange flame — an effigy of burning in a world where nothing burns unless it means something. Deleted; the turn-2 re-render puts both plumes on one honest westerly. Everything else held; annotations on 0712 (frontal deep-focus; near-hollow tiers), 0713 (hex poured paving), 0718 (menhir background) and 0720 (neutral-pearlescent horizon) are kept in the sheet, not waved through.

What the doctrine still forbids — and what this batch notes

NO crown, NO throne, NO regalia of any kind — the batch demonstrates a state in which those objects do not exist. Canals DEAD; smoke WEST everywhere (enforced twice, at reject cost); sea EAST as the chalk seam; no sun; no warm horizon (0720's pale far-right kept only as neutral pearl); NO SNOW anywhere; no text, no glyphs, no lettering anywhere — the custom is spoken and worn, never written.
