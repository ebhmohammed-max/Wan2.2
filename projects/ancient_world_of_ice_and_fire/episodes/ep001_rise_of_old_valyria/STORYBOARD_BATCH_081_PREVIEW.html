The sky answers to no one — yet

The passage that closes this batch is the most overt piece of pyromancy prose since the fire-work itself: “Where mortal masons stacked square and heavy battlements, Valyrian sorcerers twisted fused black stone into sweeping, organic spires that soared hundreds of feet aloft, opening at their crowns into wind-swept sky-courts and heated roosts built not for human footsteps, but for descending dragons.” The batch works two questions at once: what mortal ashlar was to old Valyria, and what an empty dragon-roost is to human architecture. Frames 0801–0803 keep the mortal thread — ashlar on the non-plate, the mason's broken arc, HER before the spire's shoulder; 0804–0805 scale the suite; 0806–0809 stage the throat of a sky-court from inside at three scales, under the director's new ruling (see below); 0810 opens the skyline to the ridge track BESIDE an EMPTY sky — the dragons have not arrived, and the span does not call them.

The director's canal ruling — and what it does not cover

(2026-11-13, ask_user option c): LIVE MAGMA CANAL BEDS render emissive amber-crimson for the Ch. VI set-pieces only. The canal beds may be hot; the surrounding fused stone still TAKES light and never makes it; reflected amber-crimson appears ONLY on adjacent surfaces/faces as a canal-reflection, and under a per-frame budget: one inner curve (0806, 0807), a jaw-and-cheek strip (0808), one deep pocket (0809). No frame in this batch contains a flame event. The ruling does NOT open the episode-wide doctrine (§5), it does NOT license canals elsewhere, and it does NOT retroactively legalize the renders that drift elsewhere in Ch. VI — the smoke-WEST fence shows up in this very batch (0804-v1, 0805-v1, 0810-v1/v2 all failed east-drift; 0810 needed a zero-top/zero-right hard fence plus one WEST-edge thread to pass).

Fused spires are now legally canonical in the episode lexicon: SOLID, legless, single-mass, TORN crowns — no lattice, no ovals, no arches.
