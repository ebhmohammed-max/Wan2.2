Two palettes in one batch: **black rock and one contained ember line** for the Valyrian claim, then **deep indigo and violet twilight** for Qarth.

**Continuity:** *…they proclaimed, were the literal children of the Fourteen Flames — born directly from the womb of the volcanoes, bestowed uniquely upon Valyria. Elsewhere in Essos, storytellers spun stranger fables. In the merchant palaces of Qarth, traders long repeated an enchanting myth: that two moons once hung above the earth…*

**Structure — a claim, then a fable.** `0401`–`0405` finish the Valyrian origin claim as geology: the shaft, the rock at the boundary of heat, the egg-stone in its ash cradle, the inside of the split shell, and the indifferent landscape. `0406`–`0410` then cut to **Qarth**, a location new to the production, and stage the cracked-moon fable as an evening city and a storyteller's hands. The tonal jump is the point: Valyria's answer is rock, Qarth's is poetry, and the images must feel like different countries.

**Standing directives carried forward, plus four new ones:**
- **Heat is a contained line, never a fire.** Every warm element is one dull orange-red seam inside black rock, low in frame, with a dark glassy crust across it.
- **NEW — `QARTH-MYTHIC` form lock.** Hot, ancient, ornate merchant city of **pale carved sandstone** with shallow relief carving, arched colonnades, tiered towers, lacquered wood and **silk awnings in indigo and violet**, rendered **entirely in the deep indigo and violet of late twilight**, lit by sparse **pale blue-white** lamps. No gold, no sun, no warm light, no daylight blue sky, no green grass, no modern forms.
- **NEW — the egg-stone is a stone, not an egg.** Hard overlapping plates grown into rock, char-black, edged with pale mineral deposit, split along a flaw with glassy faces. Never smooth, never pale, never a bird's egg.
- **NEW — the Qartheen are anonymous.** The storyteller is a back, a shoulder and a pair of hands; never a face, never a portrait.
- **NEW — no legible text of any kind.** Carving is geometric, vegetal and animal, and must never resolve into script, signage or numerals.

**No re-renders were required.** One potential issue was caught in review rather than in production: a small anomalous shape low in `0401` that could have been a figure in a frame that must contain none. A 2x crop showed it to be a rock edge catching the ember light.

**Lessons from this batch to carry into 042:**

1. **A batch can hold two palettes if the cut is clean.** Five frames of black rock and ember, five of indigo and violet, with no transitional frame between them. The cut itself does the narrative work of moving from one civilisation's answer to another's.
2. **Writing a new location's form lock up front works.** `QARTH-MYTHIC` had no bible entry, so its palette, materials and light were defined in the batch and repeated in every prompt — and all five frames matched. The lock is now recorded in the manifest for subsequent batches.
3. **Refusing warmth is a style.** Qarth's opulence is carried entirely by violet, indigo, cool lavender stone and pale blue-white lamps. Any gold would have made it generic; the absence of warm light is what makes it feel foreign and rich at once.
4. **The object is the cheapest way to carry an abstraction.** `0403` (an egg-stone as a birth), `0404` (the inside of a shell as a before-state) and `0410` (a cracked disc as a fable) each carry an idea the narration takes a whole sentence to state — and none of them can fail anatomy or expression.
5. **A prop in a character's hands beats a character's face.** `0410` is the only close human contact in the batch and it is entirely hands. It gives the fable a physical object without ever producing a portrait.
6. **Perfect batches come from constraints, not from luck.** Ten for ten followed two batches of explicit form locks (heat, limb count, hair, architecture) plus the standing five-part animal signature. Each rule learned in a rejection was written into every brief this time before generation.
