## SECTION A — ENVIRONMENT & ARCHITECTURAL PROFILES

### 1. `LOC-VAL-001`: THE VALYRIAN PENINSULA & THE FOURTEEN FLAMES *(Batch 001: `IMG0007–IMG0010`; Batches 002–105)*
- **Historical Period:** Pre-Doom Intact Peninsula (ca. 5,000+ BC — unbroken landmass millennia before the Smoking Sea fracture).
- **Geography & Climate:** Mountainous southern Essosi peninsula jutting into the cerulean Summer Sea between the Lands of the Long Summer (west) and the Gulf of Grief (east); dominated by a majestic crescent of fourteen towering active stratovolcanoes; warm humid coastal air meeting volcanic heat.
- **Vegetation:** Lush emerald ash-fertilized meadows, wild olives, figs, and dark cypress in lower valleys; barren black basalt scree and yellow sulfur crusts above 6,000 feet.
- **Materials & Colors:** Columnar black basalt (`#141416`), volcanic ash grey (`#3E4044`), pasture emerald (`#2D5A36`), molten magma orange-gold (`#E85D1A`), Summer Sea turquoise-blue (`#1B4D6E`).
- **Architecture:** Unspoiled geological titan-landscape in Ch. I–III, transitioning to fused-black-stone ridges and straight dragonroads in Ch. V–VII.
- **Lighting & Landmarks:** Dual-source sunlight piercing towering white-and-grey steam plumes by day and under-lit crimson caldera glow at dusk; signature crescent of three tallest central peaks overlooking a valley descending to the sea.

### 2. `LOC-VAL-002`: PASTORAL FOOTHILLS & BASALT SHEEPHERDER HOMESTEADS *(Batches 002–003: `IMG0016–IMG0030`; Batches 018–063)*
- **Historical Period:** Pre-Dragon Pastoral Era, ca. 5,000+ BC.
- **Architectural Style & Materials:** Low-slung circular and rectangular dry-stacked black volcanic basalt dwellings with weathered cedar rafters, dark slate roofs, and woven wattle sheepfolds—strictly pre-imperial, non-medieval-European pastoralism.
- **Geography, Vegetation & Lighting:** Terraced highland emerald meadows beside steaming geothermal mineral streams; morning mist sliced by golden sunbeams beneath distant smoking volcanic cones.
- **Typical Inhabitants:** Silver-haired, purple-eyed Valyrian clans (`CHAR-VAL-001`, `CHAR-VAL-002` Stage A) and flocks of long-wooled mountain sheep.

### 3. `LOC-ESS-002`: ANCIENT RIVER SETTLEMENTS OF THE MOTHER RHOYNE *(Batches 005–006: `IMG0046–0060` & Batches 008–009: `IMG0076–0090`)*
- **Historical Period:** Long Night Flashback (`IMG0046–0060`) & Post-Long Night Recovery (`IMG0076–0090`), pre-5,000 BC.
- **Architectural Style & Materials:** Graceful open river-pavilions, arched bridges, and stepped quays carved of warm pale rose-and-cream sandstone; gilded shallow-draft river skiffs; riverside clay-and-stone **iron-smelting bloomery furnaces** powered by waterwheels.
- **Geography, Climate & Colors:** Vast, tranquil silver-jade expanse of the Mother Rhoyne lined with weeping willows and misty islands; warm golden sunlight contrasting with glowing orange iron-bloomery embers (and in `IMG0046–0060`, locked in jagged blue-white winter ice beneath generation-long darkness).

### 4. `LOC-ESS-003`: ASSHAI-BY-THE-SHADOW *(Batches 010–011: `IMG0091–0105`; Batches 046–047: `IMG0451–0465`)*
- **Historical Period:** Timeless Pre-Valyrian Antiquity.
- **Architectural Style & Materials:** Cyclopean, windowless or slit-windowed stepped palaces, monolithic bridges, and harbor piers built entirely of **greasy, matte light-drinking black stone** (strictly distinct from Valyria's smoother heat-fused black stone—Asshai's stone looks slick, ancient, sharp-angled, and absorbs light).
- **Geography, Climate & Lighting:** Narrow, poison-black River Ash emerging from the towering Shadow Mountains beneath oppressive perpetual twilight gloom; zero vegetation, zero children or animals; pale ghostly grey-green mist pierced only by dim blood-red shadowbinder lanterns.

### 5. `LOC-ESS-001`: OLD GHIS — CAPITAL OF THE GHISCARI EMPIRE *(Batch 001: `IMG0006`; Batches 011–015: `IMG0106–0150`; Batches 095–105)*
- **Historical Period:** Peak Pre-Valyrian Ghiscari Dominance, pre-5,000 BC.
- **Architectural Style & Materials:** Monumental ancient Near-Eastern / Mesopotamian-inspired brickwork (*ASOS* Daenerys III): colossal **stepped pyramids of fired yellow-ochre and terracotta brick** with irrigated hanging palm terraces, massive crenellated mud-brick ramparts, glazed lapis-blue tile friezes, wide limestone parade avenues, and a **100-foot cast-bronze statue of the Harpy of Old Ghis** (woman's torso, bat wings for arms, scorpion tail, eagle talons clutching a **thunderbolt**).
- **Geography, Climate & Lighting:** Arid sun-baked eastern coast of the Gulf of Grief overlooking a harbor packed with bronze-rammed war-galleys; harsh, brilliant desert-gold sunlight casting razor-sharp geometric shadows.
- **Typical Inhabitants:** Grazdan the Great (`CHAR-GHI-001`), *tokar*-draped Ghiscari patricians, chained slave columns, and **Lockstep Legions** (tall rectangular/curved bronze-faced shields, three graduated spears, plumed bronze helms).

### 6. `LOC-VAL-003`, `LOC-VAL-004`, `LOC-VAL-005`, `LOC-VAL-006` *(Active in Batches 020–105)*
- **`LOC-VAL-003` (High Caldera Caverns):** Natural hexagonal basalt pillars, vitrified obsidian shelves, yellow sulfur crusts, glowing magma fissures.
- **`LOC-VAL-004` (Early Freehold Citadel):** Seamless, mortarless dragonflame-fused black stone spires twisting skyward into open dragon-roosts; engineered lava canals; Valyrian sphinxes (dragon bodies, human faces); First Council Hall ringed by forty bronze fire-tripods.
- **`LOC-VAL-005` (Subterranean Mines):** Suffocating volcanic ore shafts veined with gold, silver, and copper; timber-and-chain winches over magma chasms; scalding red steam vents.
- **`LOC-VAL-006` (Valyrian Dragonroad):** Arrow-straight, seamless fused grey-black stone highway raised six inches above lush green earth (*ADWD* Tyrion II).

### 7. `QARTH-MYTHIC`: QARTH, THE QUEEN OF CITIES — THE STORYTELLERS' TERRACES *(Batches 041–043: `IMG0406–0430`)*
- **Historical Period:** Ancient, fabulously wealthy Essoi merchant city, contemporaneous with pre-Doom Valyria; the eastern pole of the episode's trade world.
- **Geography & Climate:** A hot, humid harbour city on the Strait of Qarth at the southern tip of Essos, built on terraces between the sea and dry hills; flat dark water, dense shipping, long still evenings.
- **Palette — THE LOCK:** Everything is rendered in the **deep indigo and violet of late twilight**. Stone reads cool grey-lavender rather than warm sand. Light comes only from cold stars, two moons, and sparse **pale blue-white** street lamps or braziers burning **pale blue-white, never orange**. **No gold, no golden bazaars, no warm yellow light, no daylight blue sky.** Canon-readers expect a golden market; the production deliberately denies it, and every warm source must be explicitly negated in the prompt or the frame drifts to a generic desert port.
- **Architecture:** Pale carved sandstone throughout, hot and ancient and ornate: monumental gate towers sheathed in **shallow geometric, vine and animal relief**, projecting tiered balconies, arched loggias and colonnades, lacquered wood screens, domes and needle-thin towers, sagging **silk awnings in violet, indigo and dull crimson** strung between poles. Carving is abstract — **never legible script, signage or numerals**.
- **The terraces:** Raised roof terraces and palace balconies with low carved stone parapets, rugs on paving, low benches, lamp brackets and a view over the roofs to a harbour crowded with thin masts. These are the stage for the moon fable.
- **TWO MOONS — CANON, AND ONLY HERE:** For the Qartheen fable the sky holds **two moons**: one large **silvery-cream** disc with a mottled grey surface, one smaller, duller **bronze-grey** disc. Both are pale, cold and **without halo, flare or glow**, photographed as objects in an indigo sky with cold stars. Their one fracture is a **branching shell crack filled with cold white-blue light**, never an explosion. **Two moons must never appear over Valyria or any other location.**
- **People:** Qartheen merchants and listeners in long pale robes, draped violet and indigo silk, dark head-wraps, pale carved stone finger rings. **Anonymous by rule:** backs, shoulders, hands, a profile turned away — **never a portrait, never a face to the lens**. The storyteller is a voice: a back in batch 041, a shoulder and a pair of hands thereafter.
- **Narration anchor:** *"Elsewhere in Essos, storytellers spun stranger fables. In the merchant palaces of Qarth, traders long repeated an enchanting myth: that two moons once hung above the earth…"*

### 8. `COAST-OF-IB`: THE BLEAK COAST OF IB, SHIVERING SEA *(Batches 043–044: `IMG0421–0440`)*
- **Historical Period:** Uncounted antiquity. The bone field is already fossilised and half-buried when Maester Yandel records it — the dragons it belongs to died long before Valyria.
- **Geography & Climate:** A cold, treeless Arctic island coast. Black basalt shore shelves, an unbroken leaden sea to a flat horizon, wind-carved sastrugi snowfields and permafrost, frozen spray crust along the tideline. No vegetation of any kind.
- **Palette:** Desaturated cold — black rock, blue-grey sea, white-blue snow, stone-grey bone, pewter overcast sky. **Flat, shadowless, sunless light.** No warm light, no golden hour, no sunset, no sunrise, no aurora, no blue-sky sunshine, no green anywhere.
- **⚠️ SNOW AND ICE ARE PERMITTED HERE — AND ONLY HERE.** The long-standing "no snow, no ice, no frost" ban is on record as **peninsula-scoped** (`LOC-VAL-001` and its foothills). Ib is defined by its ice, so this location is the sanctioned exception. It must not be read as a general licence: **Ib is the only place in Episode 001 where snow is weather rather than a violation.**
- **The bone field — THE LOCK:** Fossilised dragon remains, mineralised to stone-dark grey-black, wet where the snow has scoured them and pale-dusted where dry, pitted, fracture-lined and veined with thin pale mineral deposit. **Only fragments, sections and arcs:** a collapsed ribcage wrecked like a hull, a buried spine of cart-wheel vertebrae, three ribs canted at broken angles, a partial skull with one deep rimed eye socket exposed, single vertebrae prised out of frozen earth. **Never a complete mounted skeleton standing upright, never a tidy symmetric arc, never a museum display** — that failure is the batch's one recorded re-render. Anatomy stays four-limbed: one pair of wings, one pair of hind legs.
- **People:** Ibbenese whalers — heavy-set, broad, in tarred dark oilskins with matted seal-fur collars, hoods pulled up and crusted with salt rime and frozen breath, bare cracked hands. Anonymous by rule: **backs, hoods, shoulders and hands only, never a face.** They work with ropes, iron pry bars, wooden tripods, sleds and skids. **No maester and no Citadel figure is ever shown** — the Citadel's interest is a voiceover fact, never a person.
- **Narration anchor:** *"Yet the Citadel could not so easily dismiss harder evidence pulled from the soil of distant lands. As Maester Yandel records, fossilized and weathered dragon bones have been unearthed thousands of leagues from Valyria—from the bleak, icy wastes of Ib in the Shivering Sea…"*

### 9. `SOTH-JUNGLE`: THE STEAMING JUNGLES OF SOTHORYOS *(Batches 044–045: `IMG0431–0450`)*
- **Historical Period:** Uncounted antiquity, the far south. The bones here are already black fossil when they are found.
- **Geography & Climate:** Steaming, plague-ridden equatorial jungle. Wet black loam, grey limestone outcrops, colossal buttress-rooted hardwoods, strangler vines, moss hanging in sodden ropes, standing black water, a permanent haze of steam. Oppressive, still and humid.
- **Palette — THE LOCK:** Deep green-brown, ochre mud, grey limestone, black water, pale steam. **Flat, diffused, steam-filtered light only.** No golden light, no sunbeams, no sun disc, no bright blue sky, no turquoise water, no beach, no bright flowers, no postcard tropics. The jungle is beautiful and it is unwell.
- **The bone:** Black fossil, sunken into wet earth, moss-taken and lichen-crusted, roots grown through the cracks and standing water pooled in the hollows. Any fractured face shows **concentric lamellae and radial fracture lines** — the detail that proves bone rather than fallen timber.
- **Wyverns — `CRT-SOTH-001` (distant only):** Smaller, fireless kin of the dragons. Rendered **only as dark silhouettes against pale vapour**, small in frame, no fire, no glow, no colour, no detail beyond membrane wings, a whip tail and a low arrowhead skull. They are animals that still live here, not heraldry.
- **People:** None. No faces, no maester, no Citadel figure, no expedition party.
- **Narration anchor:** *"…to the steaming, plague-ridden jungles of Sothoryos in the far south, where smaller, fireless kin called wyverns still hunt today."*

### 9b. ⚠️ `LOC-VAL-003` CALDERA — THE ARCHITECTURE BAN *(repeat offender: Batch 031, re-confirmed Batch 044)*
- The High Caldera Caverns must **never** render as a built cathedral. `IMG0310`, `IMG0436` and `IMG0438` all failed the same way: fluted columns with capitals, coursed ashlar, a rose-window aperture, a carved frieze, a glowing light shaft.
- **Write the rock positively:** hexagonal basalt prisms of uneven height, jagged broken tops, **no capitals, no drum sections, no plinths**, collapsed rubble and darkness where a ceiling would be, raw chaotic geological disorder at enormous scale.
- **Negate by name:** masonry courses, ashlar blocks, dressed stone, walls, arches, vaults, rose windows, carved frieze, pilasters, built symmetry, light shafts, god rays.
- The chamber is an **accident of scale and disorder**, never a nave.

### 9c. `DRG-VAL-001` — ANATOMY RISK MANAGEMENT *(learned across Batches 032–045)*
- **The recurring failure is not the count, it is the pose.** Every third-limb error in this production has come from a **crouched, detail-lit animal with its wings forward** (first at `IMG0398`, again at `IMG0446`). In that configuration the model adds a gripping extremity it can see and a hanging one it cannot.
- **Countermeasures, in order of effectiveness:**
  1. **Put it in flight** with both hind legs tucked tight against the belly and **out of sight** — nothing to miscount.
  2. **Render it as a pure silhouette.** `IMG0443` holds the lock better than any detailed frame because the outline enforces the count.
  3. **State the count numerically AND negatively:** "exactly four limbs — two taloned hind legs, two wings, no third leg, no fourth leg, no arms", plus "zero claws hang below the silhouette" and a negation of *extra limbs, third leg, fourth leg, arms, dangling feet*.
- **Still mandatory:** the 2x limb check on every frame the animal appears in, crouched or not, silhouette or not.
- **Horn tolerance (accepted):** the twin swept-back primary horns have drifted into a heavier spike crown in several approved frames (`0437`, `0442`). This is an accepted tolerance provided the swept-back direction and one dominant horn pair read clearly and the silhouette is correct. It is a recorded decision, not silent drift.

## SECTION B — DRAGON & CREATURE CONTINUITY PROFILES

> **Mandatory Book-Canon Anatomy Rule:** Every dragon in *A Song of Ice and Fire* has **four limbs total**—**two muscular taloned hind legs** and **two vast bat-like forelimb wings** (walking on calloused wing-knuckles when grounded). Six-limbed dragons (four legs + two wings) are strictly forbidden. Zero non-canon named dragons are invented.

### 1. `DRG-VAL-001`: ELDER WILD CALDERA DRAGON *(Unnamed Period Design — Batches 032–045)*
- **Age & Scale:** ~140 years old · 150 ft length · 210 ft wingspan.
- **Coloration & Anatomy:** Strictly 4-limbed; volcanic basalt-black and oxidized charcoal scales (`#1A1B1E`) with ember-orange incandescent throat fissures when breathing flame; twin swept-back curved obsidian horns; molten brass-gold vertical-slit eyes; sulfur-crusted nostrils; signature semicircular healed tear-scar in right outer leathery wing membrane.

### 2. `DRG-VAL-002`: FIRST-BOUND VALYRIAN MOUNT *(Unnamed Period Design — Batches 050–105)*

IV–VII with archaic dark-iron and heat-cured ox-hide neck riding chains/straps (no late-era saddle).

### 3. `CRT-VAL-001`: SUBTERRANEAN FIREWYRM *(Canon Creature — Batches 022–023 & 089–090)*
- **Source:** *AFFC* Arya II; *F&B*.
- **Anatomy & Traits:** Completely **wingless and legless** cylindrical burrowing fire-breather (up to 40+ ft in deep shafts); pale blistered bone-white to magma-charred armor rings; blind heat-sensing head plates; crystalline rock-crushing maw glowing white-hot inside; bores circular tunnels through solid volcanic rock.
