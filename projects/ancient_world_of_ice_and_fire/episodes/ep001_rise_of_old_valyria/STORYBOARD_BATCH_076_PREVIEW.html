The race begins — with nothing yet standing

The batch renders the Freehold diverging from a common agrarian life toward monumental rivalry, without yet naming a capital. 0751 opens on the first fresh mound now risen out of frame and the HIS/HER profiles; 0752 puts the rivalry at ground level: two rope gangs pulling at one rift-shelf, neither gang acknowledging the other; 0753 widens to the whole-day panorama — dozens of holds digging at once; 0754 counts the forty from above, everyone equally unfinished; 0755 narrows to the DISCIPLINE of a rival plan over a BLANK maquette — HIS intent visible in his hands; 0756 the DRAGON YARD — the animals already here, chained and grounded; 0757 HER hand resting on the inert objects — the objects carry the sorcery, never her eyes; 0758 the rival WORK-CREWS at one rift at once — the jealous parity in its purest form; 0759 the ordinary life beneath — sheepfolds and timber halls, the only wooden roofs anywhere in the episode so far; 0760 the same ground with the FIRST CAPITAL-SCALE POUR already laid — the old world in the bottom third, the new pour across the center, no name for either yet.
