This note SETS BINDING CRAFT LAW for all remaining batches (087 onward) and any future revisions. Prior documents remain as historical record; they are NOT retro-edited. ## 1. The Valyrian peoples are not ordinary humans (effective immediately)
The FORTY HOUSES, Freehold dragonlord patricians, and free Valyrian citizens MUST NOT read as an earthly council — no generic Mediterranean peasant-council, no village-elder bonhomie, no anonymous crowd. Valyrians are a race apart, born beside fire, and the camera must say so at a glance.

**Mandatory VALYRIAN ATTRIBUTES (ALL Valyrian characters, noble or common, unless explicitly downplayed by a specific brief):**
- **Hair — silver-gold / platinum / white-gold**, near-monotone falls or manes; NO brown, black, chestnut, auburn or "earth man" hair on any Valyrian (Ghiscari, Rhoynar and foreigners in-frame may differ — see §3).
- **Eyes — violet / amethyst / lilac / pale-lilac spectrum** (CLM-009 in force; the spectrum runs pale lilac-grey through true amethyst — never HBO cartoon purple, never brown).
- **Face/form — narrow-faced beauty bordering on inhuman**: high cheekbones, almond eyes, a languid stillness; tall; poised; the alien grace of people whose ancestors rode dragons. NOT weathered, NOT broad-featured, NOT sun-cured peasant.
- **Demeanor — arrogance, languor, containment.** Valyrian lords do not gesture; they do not hurry; contempt is their resting face. Wine is taken in long silences, not toasts between rough equals.

## 2. The custom is ALIEN SPLENDOR, not folksy gathering
Wine, councils, processions and gatherings remain REQUIRED (standing 2026-11-13 directive) — but they must stage as **a convocation of near-immortal dragonlords**: black glass, obsidian, tarnished gold (GOLD-DULL), bronze, unmarked torcs, amethysts, NO crowns (unchanged), silence and symmetry of bearing, beauty that unsettles. The horror of the Freehold is that its magnificence is real and its cruelty sublime; it is never rustic.

## 3. Scope of the rule
- **Dragonlords / the forty houses / Freehold patricians / their kin and children:** full attributes, no exceptions.
- **Free Valyrian commoners:** attributes diluted but present — silver-streaked or ash-pale hair, pale-lilac grey eyes, finer features than an earth peasant. NEVER an earthy everyman.
- **Foreigners in frame** (Ghiscari, Rhoynar, enslaved peoples, mercenary types) MAY carry earth tones and earth features — the CONTRAST is the point; batch 088+ exploits it.
- HER (`CHAR-VAL-002`) and HIS (`CHAR-VAL-003`/`CHAR-VAL-001`) locked kits already satisfy this note and remain unchanged.
