The arc of the warning — and the answer

0701 accuses the over-reacher — a monolith too tall and one dragon with no peers. 0702 shows the emblem of that reach: a blank overlord-boss, unmarked forever. 0703 lands the arithmetic on his face — amethyst hard WEST, certainty without appetite — 0704 shows the hand that withholds. 0705 stops time at the exact breath before annihilation (every western smoke converged into one iron seam; ~11 unlit dragons riding above it). 0706 begins the answer in masonry — the unroofed landholder halls. 0707 carves the pride as parity — equal plinths, equal elders, nothing engraved. 0708 lays the arcs flat around a bare decision-stone. 0709 lifts off for the full geography — many separate holds like vertebrae, alone in the pasture the assembly shell goes up. 0710 answers the question of who the Freehold is FOR: an elder matriarch of no dragon-lineage line at all, eyes closed, waiting.

What failed, honestly

0709 v1 rendered one unified dark mandala-metropolis — continuous walls, dense fabric, dozens of smokes inside a single bounded fabric. It was beautiful and wrong: the state ALREADY BUILT, in the wrong tense — the batch's brief is the prospective founding, the state that does not exist yet. Deleted; the turn-2 re-prompt (many separate vertebrae-holds, NO walls, the empty pasture between, the arc alone under construction) fixed it — and arguably made the batch's money frame. Everything else held; nothing here was waved through: 0703 carries the epic's FIRST BEARD unrequested, deliberate-age class; 0704 the tiny red diamond on the signet's shoulder is flagged for your ruling; 0705's dragon chain reads as distinct wings rather than a murk; 0706/0707 lean near-rectangular; 0708/0710 render the bench stone light-grey rough-cut.
