## 1. SEVEN-DOMAIN CONTINUITY AUDIT

1. **Timeline Continuity:** Separates Dawn-Age / Long Night legends (~8,000–6,000 BC) from the Rise of Valyria (~5,000 BC) with explicit Citadel chronological caveats (*TWOIAF*; *ASOS* Samwell I). Stops precisely on the eve of the First Ghiscari War (`IMG1050`) so Episode 002 owns the five Ghiscari Wars in full.
2. **Character Continuity:** Zero fabricated 5,000 BC Valyrian personal names. Only canon-attested **Grazdan the Great** (`CHAR-GHI-001`) is named in VO; recurring Valyrian/Asshai'i leads (`CHAR-VAL-001`, `CHAR-VAL-002`, `CHAR-VAL-003`, `CHAR-ASH-001`) use locked 22-field Identity Sheets and Reference Plates (`REF_CHAR_VAL_001`, `REF_CHAR_GHI_001`). `CHAR-VAL-002`'s left-jawline heat scar is acquired at `IMG0526` (`17:30`) and locked through `IMG1005`.
3. **Location Continuity:** `LOC-VAL-001` depicts an **intact pre-Doom continental peninsula** anchored by real foreground geology and atmospheric distance haze (never the shattered Smoking Sea and never a miniature floating island). `LOC-ESS-003` (Asshai) uses **dull, matte, greasy light-drinking black stone**, kept visually distinct from `LOC-VAL-004` (Valyria's smooth, mortarless **dragonflame-fused black stone** and lava canals).
4. **Clothing Continuity:** Pastoral Valyrians wear homespun undyed wool, sheepskin, and native copper pins; Ghiscari patricians wear gold-fringed **`tokars`** draped over the left arm with oiled horned hair (*ASOS*, *ADWD*).
5. **Architectural Continuity:** Valyria evolves organically from stacked basalt sheepfolds (`LOC-VAL-002`) to twisted seamless black spires, sphinxes, and 6-inch-raised straight dragonroads (`LOC-VAL-004`, `LOC-VAL-006`). Old Ghis (`LOC-ESS-001`) uses weathered sun-baked **mud-brick walls and a colossal stepped baked-brick pyramid** with faded lapis-blue glazed tile bands and a **Bronze Harpy with bat-wings in place of arms clutching a thunderbolt** (*ASOS* Daenerys III).
6. **Dragon & Creature Continuity:** All dragons (`DRG-VAL-001`, `DRG-VAL-002`) are unnamed and enforce **strictly four limbs** (two hind legs + two bat-like forelimb wings). Subterranean **firewyrms** (`CRT-VAL-001`) are **wingless, blind rock-borers** (*AFFC* Arya II).
7. **65mm Live-Action Color & Rapid-Cut Editing Continuity (`30 Images/Min`):** All 10 frames of Batch 001 (`00:00–00:20`) share unified **warm sandstone highlights + muted earth/stone/bronze midtones + deep ink-navy shadows** while varying focal length and camera elevation (`50mm → 85mm → 35mm → 40mm → 28mm → 35mm → 50mm → 28mm → 35mm → 24mm`) to prevent jump-cuts across rapid 2.0-second transitions.
