**Director's decision — the absence is the subject.** `IMG0281`–`IMG0285` are deliberately the emptiest frames of the episode: **no writing, no carving, no tally marks, no runes, no beads, no bone tokens, no parchment, no maps, no inscribed stone.** Nothing in these five frames may imply literacy or record-keeping, because the narration is explicitly about the *failure* of the record. Where earlier batches would have reached for a prop, this batch reaches for weather, rock and wool. `IMG0284` in particular is a trap: it is the "chronicle" beat, and the correct answer is a bare scraped bone and loose fleece with **nothing marked on either**.

**Where the peninsula changes:** `IMG0286`–`IMG0290` cross onto the upper edge of `LOC-VAL-002` where the pasture meets `LOC-VAL-003` — the high caldera vents. Seen from outside, in daylight, these read as a field of natural hexagonal basalt columns and yellow sulphur crust with thin steam, **not** as a cave interior and **not** as architecture. No glowing magma, no cut stone, no worked masonry: the columns are geological, the vents are geological, and the covenant is with something nobody built.

**Standing rules carried in from Batches 026–028:**
- **Wide-shot eye rule:** at 24–40mm the violet iris greys out, so every frame wider than a 50mm medium close-up keeps faces in profile, bowed to work or turned away. Iris colour is never stated in this batch; `IMG0290` is the one 50mm and its subject is looking down at the ground.
- **Hair stated positively, always:** every figure in every frame is described with long loose or half-bound silver-gold hair. The shaved-side failure has now recurred twice (Batches 027 and 028); banning it is not enough, so the positive description is written into each prompt.
- **No fencing above waist height.** Batches 027–028 both produced wattle panels standing on end that read as a gallows; anything vertical and skeletal is negated outright.
- **No snow or ice anywhere.** Batch 028 drifted into an alpine ridge; the peninsula is green, black and grey, never white.
- **No clothing darker than charcoal**, and crowds get their palette spelled out per person.
