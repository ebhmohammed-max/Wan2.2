## 3. VIDEO DESCRIPTION

Long before the Iron Throne was forged, before Aegon Targaryen crossed the Narrow Sea, and thousands of years before the events of *Game of Thrones*, a quiet community of silver-haired shepherds grazed their flocks beneath fourteen active volcanoes on a southern peninsula of Essos.

How did an isolated pastoral people without kings, legions, or walled cities master living dragons, liquefy stone like molten wax, and forge the Valyrian Freehold—eclipsing ancient superpowers like the Old Empire of Ghis? And what dark mysteries in the annals of Asshai-by-the-Shadow and Septon Barth's *Unnatural History* still surround their rise?

Welcome to **Episode 001** of ***The Ancient World of Ice and Fire***—a chronological, book-canon cinematic documentary series exploring the complete history of George R. R. Martin's universe from the Dawn Age and the Rise of Old Valyria to the fall of the Targaryen dynasty.
