**The lesson this batch adds to the arc:** the no-face rule is not a framing instruction, it is a **crop check**, and it now has a second clause. The head must be *outside* the frame, not merely turned; and the fallback "just show a shoulder or a hand instead" has its own failure mode — **skin at the edge of frame is still a body, and a body without its robe is a continuity error, not a camera angle.** The third pass of `0548` proves the reliable way out: photograph the wear, not the wearer.

**Second lesson:** `0546`'s flame had to be *contained in a vessel of stone* to survive the batch's own rule. Fire is allowed in this episode only where a hand built a place to hold it, and the frame must show the containment as clearly as the fire.
