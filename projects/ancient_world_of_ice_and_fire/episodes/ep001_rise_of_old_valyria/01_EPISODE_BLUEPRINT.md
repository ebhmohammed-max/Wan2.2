## 2. CHRONOLOGICAL TIMELINE COVERED IN EPISODE 001

1. **Dawn Age & The Long Night (ca. 8,000–6,000 BC, Legendary Context):** Essosi memories of the generation-long darkness—the freezing of the upper Rhoyne tributaries, eastern traditions of the Blood Betrayal, and Citadel uncertainty over ancient chronology.
2. **Pre-Valyrian Essos (Pre-5,000 BC):**
   - Along the **Mother Rhoyne**, river-communities pioneer early ironworking, music, and riverine trade.
   - In the Far East beyond the Bone Mountains, **Yi Ti** rises in the shadow of older myths while **Asshai-by-the-Shadow** endures in greasy, light-drinking black stone.
   - On the Gulf of Grief, **Grazdan the Great** establishes the **Old Empire of Ghis**, conquering neighbors with disciplined **Lockstep Legions** and mass slavery beneath the Harpy clutching a thunderbolt.
3. **Pastoral Valyria (ca. 5,000+ BC):** Isolated, silver-haired, purple-eyed sheepherders tend flocks in the fertile ash valleys beneath the **Fourteen Flames**, surrounded by untapped veins of copper, tin, iron, silver, and gold—and subterranean **firewyrms**.
4. **Discovery in the High Caverns (ca. 5,000+ BC):** Valyrian shepherds encounter wild **dragons** nesting within the volcanic calderas; examination of the three canonical origin traditions (Valyrian volcanic birth, Qartheen cracked-moon myth, and global prehistoric dragon bones from Ib to Sothoryos).
5. **The Mystery of Dragon-Binding (ca. 5,000+ BC):** Contrast between Valyrian self-mastery claims and the Asshai'i account of nameless teachers from the Shadow; binding dragons through sorcerous glyph-banded horns, fire-spells, and suspected blood magic (Septon Barth's *Unnatural History*).
6. **Birth of the Valyrian Freehold (ca. 5,000 BC):** Emergence of the **forty noble dragonlord families**, institutionalization of incestuous blood-purity marriage, rejection of monarchy in favor of a freeholder republic led by rival dragonlords and temporary **archons**.
7. **Fused Black Stone & Subterranean Bondage (ca. 5,000 BC):** Dragonflame and pyromancy liquefy stone into seamless black towers, sphinxes, and straight **dragonroads**, while hunger for ore beneath the Fourteen Flames initiates brutal subterranean mining slavery.
8. **Threshold of War (ca. 5,000 BC → Episode 002 Transition):** Old Ghis looks west across the Gulf of Grief with envy and alarm at Valyria's mines and winged monsters, setting the stage for the **First Ghiscari War**.

## 3. KEY CHARACTERS & ARCHETYPAL CONTINUITY ANCHORS

*Note on Canon Integrity (Section 2 & Section 7):* Because George R. R. Martin recorded no individual Valyrian personal names from 5,000 BC, only historically attested figures (**Grazdan the Great**) carry personal names in narration. Early Valyrians and Asshai'i figures are portrayed visually through permanently locked **Unnamed Canonical Archetypes** (`CHAR-VAL-001`, `CHAR-VAL-002`, `CHAR-VAL-003`, `CHAR-ASH-001`) so human visual continuity remains 100% consistent without inventing non-canon historical biographies.

- **`CHAR-GHI-001` — Grazdan the Great (Canon Named Figure):** Founder of the Old Empire of Ghis and creator of the Lockstep Legions.
- **`CHAR-VAL-001` — The Elder Valyrian Shepherd (Unnamed Canon Archetype):** Pre-dragon pastoral flock-master of the lower Fourteen Flames valleys.
- **`CHAR-VAL-002` — The Young Valyrian Seeker / First Rider (Unnamed Canon Archetype):** Traces the historical arc from mountainside herdsman (`Stage A`) to cavern dragon-tamer (`Stage B`) to early Freehold dragonlord (`Stage C`).
- **`CHAR-VAL-003` — The Early Valyrian Fire-Mage & Blood-Shaper (Unnamed Canon Archetype):** Early Freehold sorceress overseeing blood-rites and the shaping of fused black stone.
- **`CHAR-ASH-001` — The Teacher from the Shadow (Disputed Legend Archetype):** Visualizes the unverified Asshai'i chronicle of nameless dragon-tamers from the Shadow Lands.

## 6. CENTRAL CONFLICT & EPISODE OBJECTIVE

- **Central Conflict:** How an isolated, non-martial community of shepherds living atop the most volatile volcanic chain on earth confronted apex fire-breathing predators, unlocked forbidden arts of horn, flame, and blood, and erected a kingless republic of dragonlords whose hunger for subterranean wealth placed both human liberty and the ancient Empire of Old Ghis in mortal peril.
- **Episode Objective:** Immerse the viewer in pre-Valyrian Essos and the birth of Old Valyria without fabricating non-canon names or resolving mysteries George R. R. Martin intentionally left ambiguous—establishing the foundational timeline, visual language, character/environment continuity, and geopolitical tension that launches Episode 002 (*The Ghiscari Wars*).
