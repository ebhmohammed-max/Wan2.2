Batch 039 · 12:40–13:00 · frames IMG0381–IMG0390 · they learned when the great flyers hunted fish and leviathans over the Summer Sea, and when they slept gorged upon cavern shelves. They observed clutches of hard, scale-textured eggs nestled directly inside geothermal ash-beds, watching winged hatchlings no larger than hounds claw free of smoking shells and hiss at the mountain sky.
One location, ten frames, and a birth. The adult at full power, the eggs as objects, the nesting basin at scale, and then the hatching itself — a sequence that escalates by scale of subject rather than amount of detail, which is also why it is the cleanest batch of the episode. Eleven generations for ten frames; the single rejection was a hatchling that came back as a glossy figurine, fixed by shooting it as a head-and-shoulder portrait instead of a whole body.
