The hinge, held

This is the last cold batch before the episode lights the kiln. The chapter opens on the elsewhere-world’s arithmetic — “Old Ghis stacked millions of baked bricks into stepped pyramids; river-masons along the Rhoyne dressed blocks of rose sandstone” — and the episode lets that world exist ONLY as narration plus ONE dry baked brick in HER hand. No pyramids, no rose sandstone, no Rhoyne, no Ghis in frame, ever. Instead the camera turns to the one thing no mason had reason to chisel yet: raw Valyrian volcanic rock. 0771 she carries the brick to the rim — 0772 the weighed verdict in cupped hands — 0773 behind her: the single-mass heap-mounds rag-heaped and alive with refusal — 0774 the cast basalt monolith dead flat, alone, untouched — 0775 the torn dormant volcano, HIS crimson + HER charcoal looking up — 0776 the mountain above her upturned face — 0777 the untouched black basalt macro — 0778 the two-species gaze held — 0779 the verdict delivered at her fingertip — 0780 the bridge into the kiln-era, the long walk to the foot of the mountain.
