## The rule for this batch: the bond as evidence, never as spectacle

The narration has just reached the one clause of Barth's account it is prepared to stand behind:

> *“What history confirms beyond dispute is the result: a bond in which one rider claimed one living dragon—and the dragon accepted no other living rider while its master breathed.”*

Everything before the word *is* is speculation (*“we cannot know whether … or …”*). The word *is* is the hinge. The dramatization (19:00, `IMG0571+`, *“Imagine the morning upon the Fourteen Flames”*, still Chapter IV) comes next — bare hands on copper-bronze scales — but **this batch must not go there first**. It is the last batch of the claim's register, and it renders only the **evidence for the claim**, never the claim being lived.

**So the batch is built on three devices, in rotation:**

1. **The bond as an object.** The strap, the ring, the fitting — the technology of one-body binding, worn by one hand, useless for two.
2. **The bond as a fact of bodies.** `DRG-VAL-002` (28–33 years, 65 ft, bronze scales, ash-grey belly plates, twin metallic-bronze horns, three-ridged brow crest) rendered with the anatomy lock held: **strictly four limbs**, in flight or pure silhouette, never the crouched wings-forward configuration.
3. **The bond as a landscape / a residue.** Places where the claim's cost is legible — and one place it is visibly absent (the unused fitting).

**Hard bans for all ten frames:** no face in any frame, human or otherwise; no blood, no wound, no injury; no ceremony, procession or spectacle; no centred master; no glowing script, sigils, runes or text anywhere; no six-limbed dragons, no third leg, no arms, no dangling feet; nothing alight beyond a dull contained vent glow off-frame (rendered as red-black rock, never as light source on figures). **No human figure appears in any frame of this batch** — the rider is the subject of the claim, not a subject of frame.
