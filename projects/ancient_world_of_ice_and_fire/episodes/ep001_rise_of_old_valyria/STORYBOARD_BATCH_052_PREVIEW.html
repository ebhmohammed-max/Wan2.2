Episode 001 — Rise of Old Valyria

Batch 052 · 17:00–17:20 · IMG0511–IMG0520 · First came the horns. Hollowed from the curved horns of giant dragons or wrought of black iron and banded in red gold…
The first manufactured objects in the episode, after six batches of geology, and the argument is that they look used, not treasured. Dull matte black, ridged, chipped, stained, bound in hammered gold tarnished except where hands have worn it bright. 0517 is the best material frame of the production — iron, gold and horn separated by texture alone, with one scored line that means nothing. 0518 is one horn as long as a small boat, scored end to end and unreadable at 5x. 0520 puts a horn back into the geology it came from: small, dark, nobody near it. 0514 rejected for coursed ashlar where Valyria is poured monolith; 0519 for stars and a burning city; 0512 simply failed to render.
