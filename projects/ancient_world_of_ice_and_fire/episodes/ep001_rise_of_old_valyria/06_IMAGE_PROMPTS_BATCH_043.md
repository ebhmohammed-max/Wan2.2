**The failure worth recording: a complete skeleton is a museum, not a fossil.** `0421`'s first pass produced everything the brief asked for except the one thing that mattered — the ribs formed a **complete, symmetric, upright arc**, which reads as a mounted specimen in a hall rather than remains lying in permafrost. Fossil bone does not hold its shape; it collapses, scatters and half-buries. The fix was to write **collapse, asymmetry and incompleteness positively** ("ribs at wild uneven angles, most snapped or sunk, one still attached to three fused vertebrae, the rest scattered like the hull of a wrecked ship") and to negate the display explicitly ("nothing stands upright in a tidy arc, nothing is complete, no symmetry and no repeating pattern"). This is the same lesson as the bald elder and the shaved braid: **a negative prohibition is weaker than a positive description of the correct state.**

**Lessons from this batch to carry into 044:**

1. **"Fossilised" is a material instruction, not a mood.** The approved frames read as stone because the prompt specified wet-scoured versus dry-dusted faces, fracture lines and pale mineral veining. Bone that is merely "old" renders as clean white plastic.
2. **Scale needs one object and one human.** `0424` works because a single rib is set against six men reduced to specks and an empty snowfield. Crowds of bones create clutter; one bone and one figure create size.
3. **Labour is the most humanising action available without a face.** `0426` — six men leaning into ropes — is the only sustained physical work in the episode, and the anonymity rule costs nothing because effort reads in the back and the shoulder.
4. **A cap is a scheduling fact, not a quality one.** `0430` was not compromised, it was deferred. It is recorded as pending with its brief and its Drive slot waiting, not quietly dropped or substituted with a weaker frame.
5. **Peninsula-scoped bans need their scope restated at every new location.** Snow was legal here, but only because the original ban was recorded as peninsula-specific. Any new location must justify its palette against the ban list rather than assume silence means permission.
6. **The cut from fable to evidence is doing narrative work.** Two moons and a splitting sky, then a frozen shore and a rope. The hard tonal break is what makes the Citadel's scepticism feel reasonable and the bones feel undeniable.
