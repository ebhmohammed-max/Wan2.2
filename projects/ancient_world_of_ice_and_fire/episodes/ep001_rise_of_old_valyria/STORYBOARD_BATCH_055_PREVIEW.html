Batch principle (bible §4h). The past is never rendered as truth, only as testimony. Barth is the frame, not the subject: a wall the spells were spoken against, an empty rectangle of clean wood where a horn was lifted away, a city four thousand years dead, one woman identified by the back of her braided head and a soot-grimed hand. No glowing script, no floating glyphs, no altar tableau.
