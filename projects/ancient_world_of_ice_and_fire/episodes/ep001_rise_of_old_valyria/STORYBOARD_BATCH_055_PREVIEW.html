The lesson this batch adds

The no-face rule is a CROP check, not a framing instruction, and it has a second clause: skin at the edge of frame is still a body, and a body without its robe is a continuity error rather than a camera angle. 0548 needed three passes — ear at 3x, then bare shoulder at 2.5x — before the reliable answer appeared: photograph the wear, not the wearer. The empty pushed-back stool is stronger than any figure and satisfies testimony perfectly.

Fire is permitted only where a hand built a place to hold it, and the frame must show the containment as clearly as the fire. 0546's burning manuscripts survive the batch's no-glow rule because every flame is hand-sized and inside a stone-lined font; no blaze, no embers, no glow in the ash.
