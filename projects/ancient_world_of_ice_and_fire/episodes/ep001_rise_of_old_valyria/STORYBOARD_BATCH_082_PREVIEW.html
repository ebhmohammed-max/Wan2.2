The sphinxes enter the story

0818–0820 are the episode's first frames of the colossal Valyrian sphinxes: crouched dragon-bodies fused from single masses — wandering fracture lace only, NO courses anywhere — crowned with SERENE HUMAN faces, alternating MAN/WOMAN down the file, half-open violet eyes. This is the carved counterpoint to living dragons: the builders were ALREADY thinking in dragon bodies before the animals arrived. The doctrine fight in this batch was over faces — 0820-v1 rendered Anubis-class beast-heads and it was deleted; the approved frame holds angular HUMAN profiles in silhouette down the entire avenue. The distant gate at the axis is deliberately two opposed DRAGON-SKULL masses with a torn void — never an arch.
