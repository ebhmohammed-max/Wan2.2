## 2. `CHAR-VAL-002` — THE YOUNG VALYRIAN HERDSMAN / FIRST DRAGON-SEEKER ("THE ASCENDANT")

- **Weapons:** *Stage A:* Bronze-tipped mountain spear. *Stage C:* Early spell-tempered dark bronze/iron longsword at hip with coiled wingless-wyrm pommel
- **Family Insignia:** Archaic three-peaked volcano-and-wing motif embossed on clasp (*Stage C*, strictly non-Targaryen generic early Freehold motif)
- **Personality:** Ambitious, fearless, relentlessly curious, magnetized by fire and height
- **Social Status:** Pastoral herdsman → First-generation Dragonlord Freeholder
- **Cultural Identity:** Early Valyrian
