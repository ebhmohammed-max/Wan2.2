# E. DRAGON & CREATURE CONTINUITY DATABASE
**Database Module:** `E_DRAGON_DATABASE`  
**Canon Standard (Section 9):**
1. **Never invent a named dragon and present it as canon.** Because zero named dragons survive in George R. R. Martin's canon from 5,000 BC, all dragons in Episode 001 are explicitly registered as **Unnamed Period-Authentic Canon Designs** (`DRG-VAL-001`, `DRG-VAL-002`). Canonical named dragons (e.g., *Balerion*, *Meraxes*, *Vhagar*) are reserved strictly for their historically accurate episodes millennia later.
2. **Mandatory GRRM Anatomy Rule:** Every dragon possesses **exactly four limbs**—**two powerful hind legs** and **two vast bat-like forelimb wings** (the wing phalanges and thumb-claw form the forelimb when walking on the ground, like a bat or pterosaur). **NEVER depict six-limbed (four legs + two wings) dragons.**

## 1. `DRG-VAL-001` — THE ELDER WILD APEX DRAGON OF THE FOURTEEN FLAMES
*Primary wild cavern leviathan encountered by early Valyrian shepherds in Chapter III.*

- **Dragon Name:** Unnamed in Canon (Production ID: `DRG-VAL-001` — *Elder Wild Caldera Dragon*)
- **Approximate Age:** ~140 years old (ancient, wild-grown without captivity constraints)
- **Size:** ~150 feet (46 meters) snout-to-tail; ~210-foot (64-meter) wingspan
- **Physical Proportions:** Massive barrel-ribbed thoracic cavity housing twin furnace lungs; long sinuous muscular serpentine neck; heavy crocodilian-theropod skull; **strictly four-limbed** (two heavy taloned hind legs + two colossal forelimb wings walking on calloused wing-knuckles)
- **Scale Coloration:** Deep volcanic basalt-black and oxidized iron-charcoal (`#1A1B1E`), with faint ember-orange incandescent heat glowing between overlapping throat and chest scutes immediately prior to exhaling flame
- **Horn Structure:** Twin massive swept-back curved black obsidian-colored primary horns rising from the back of the skull, flanked by a jagged crown of smaller secondary cheek-spikes
- **Wing Structure:** Vast leathery dark slate-black wing membranes stretched across four elongated skeletal wing-fingers and a hooked thumb-claw; right outer membrane bears an authentic semicircular healed tear-scar from territorial combat with rival wild dragons
- **Tail Characteristics:** Long, muscular counterbalancing whip-tail lined along the dorsal ridge with triangular dark-iron osteoderm spines tapering to a razor spade-tip
- **Eye Appearance:** Large recessedreptilian eyes with vertical slit pupils glowing like molten brass-gold (`#E59B22`)
- **Distinctive Markings:** Pale grey volcanic sulfur-ash crusted in the crevices of the neck plates; healed claw-rakes across the left shoulder scutes; healed notch in the right outer wing membrane
- **Known Rider:** None (untamed wild apex predator of the Fourteen Flames)
- **Historical Period:** Pre-Freehold Era of Discovery, ca. 5,000+ BC
- **Established Abilities and Characteristics:** Breathes torrential black-and-orange dragonfire hot enough to vitrify basalt into black glass; roosts in high sulfurous magma caverns; preys upon mountain flocks and wild beasts of the peninsula

## 2. `DRG-VAL-002` — THE FIRST-BOUND VALYRIAN MOUNT
*Younger adult dragon bound via horn, spell, and blood-rite by `CHAR-VAL-002` in Chapters IV–VII.*

- **Dragon Name:** Unnamed in Canon (Production ID: `DRG-VAL-002` — *First-Bound Bronze-Scale Mount*)
- **Approximate Age:** ~28–33 years old across Episode 001
- **Size:** ~65 feet (20 meters) snout-to-tail; ~95-foot (29-meter) wingspan (sleek, fast, mountable young adult)
- **Physical Proportions:** Lithe, aerodynamic raptor-serpentine build; long arched neck; **strictly four-limbed** (two muscular taloned hind legs + two powerful bat-like forelimb wings)
- **Scale Coloration:** Burnished volcanic bronze and hammered-copper dorsal scales (`#8C522B`) transitioning to dark smoky ash-grey ventral belly plates (`#2E2D2B`)
- **Horn Structure:** Sleek swept-back twin metallic-bronze horns with dark charcoal tips and a pronounced three-ridged nasal/brow crest
- **Wing Structure:** Intact, supple, semi-translucent dark amber-and-umbral leathery membranes revealing fine blood-vessel branching when backlit by sun or lava
- **Tail Characteristics:** Sinuous, agile rudder-tail lined with low swept-back bronze dorsal ridges
- **Eye Appearance:** Piercing molten copper-orange eyes (`#D95B18`) with vertical black slit pupils
- **Distinctive Markings:** Dark soot-black chevron banding along the base of the neck where archaic dark-iron and fire-cured ox-hide riding chains/straps are fitted in Chapters IV–VII (note: primitive harness wrapped around natural neck spines—NOT a late-era Westerosi leather saddle!)
- **Known Rider:** `CHAR-VAL-002` (*The Young Valyrian Seeker / First Rider Archetype*)
- **Historical Period:** Transition to Early Valyrian Freehold, ca. 5,000 BC
- **Established Abilities and Characteristics:** Agile high-altitude climber; breathes vivid copper-gold flame; responds to glyph-banded dragonbinder horn resonance and Valyrian blood-speech; also assists pyromancers in liquefying stone foundations in Chapter VI

## 3. `CRT-VAL-001` — THE FIREWYRM OF THE FOURTEEN FLAMES (CANON SUBTERRANEAN CREATURE)
*Confirmed book-canon creature (`AFFC` Arya II; `F&B` Septon Barth / Aerea Targaryen).*

- **Creature Name & ID:** `CRT-VAL-001` — Subterranean Firewyrm of the Fourteen Flames
- **Approximate Age / Growth:** Ranges from pale hatchlings no thicker than a child's arm to ancient deep-earth monstrosities 40+ feet long
- **Physical Proportions:** **Completely wingless and legless** (or vestigial-limbed serpentine burrower—per *AFFC* Arya II: *"Firewyrms. Some say they are akin to dragons, for wyrms breathe fire too. Instead of soaring through the sky, they bore through stone and soil"*); elongated, heavily muscled cylindrical body built for boring through superheated rock
- **Scale / Hide Coloration:** Pale, translucent blistered bone-white to deep magma-charred ochre armor-rings coated in fine molten rock dust
- **Head Structure:** Blind or pinhole heat-sensing eyes recessed behind heavy armored boring plates; circular/hinged maw lined with basalt-crushing crystalline teeth glowing white-hot within the gullet
- **Historical Period:** Immemorial inhabitant of the Fourteen Flames (ca. 5,000+ BC through post-Doom Valyria)
- **Established Abilities and Characteristics:** Breathes intense concentrated fire and superheated steam capable of softening solid rock; bores labyrinthine circular tunnels beneath the Fourteen Flames; lethal hazard to subterranean miners
