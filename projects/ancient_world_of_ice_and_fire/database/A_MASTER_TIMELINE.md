# A. MASTER TIMELINE — THE ANCIENT WORLD OF ICE AND FIRE
**Database Module:** `A_MASTER_TIMELINE`  
**Canon Standard:** Book Canon Priority (*The World of Ice & Fire*, *Fire & Blood*, *ASOIAF*)  
**Dating Convention:** `BC` = Before Aegon's Conquest · `AC` = After Aegon's Conquest. All pre-Andal / pre-Freehold dates carry Citadel scholarly uncertainty (`ca.`).
