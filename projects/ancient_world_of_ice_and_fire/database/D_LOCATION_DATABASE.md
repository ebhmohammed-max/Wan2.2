# D. LOCATION & ARCHITECTURAL DATABASE
**Database Module:** `D_LOCATION_DATABASE`  
**Continuity Rule (Section 8):** Every major environment has a locked geological, architectural, material, lighting, and cultural profile. Ancient Valyria must NEVER resemble generic medieval European stone castles.

---

## 1. `LOC-VAL-001` — THE VALYRIAN PENINSULA & THE FOURTEEN FLAMES (MACRO-GEOGRAPHY)
- **Location Name & ID:** `LOC-VAL-001` — The Valyrian Peninsula & The Fourteen Flames
- **Historical Period:** Pre-Doom Antiquity (ca. 5,000 BC — intact peninsula, millennia before the fracturing into the Smoking Sea!)
- **Geography:** Vast, mountainous peninsula jutting southward into the turquoise **Summer Sea**, bordered on the west by the fertile coastal plains below the **Lands of the Long Summer** and on the east by the **Gulf of Grief** (Slaver's Bay). Dominated across its spine by an immense horseshoe chain of **fourteen colossal active stratovolcanoes** (**The Fourteen Flames**).
- **Climate:** Warm, humid Mediterranean-to-subtropical coastal air meeting intense geothermal heat at higher elevations; endless summer warmth.
- **Vegetation:** Lush emerald-green volcanic ash pastures, wild olive groves, cypress, figs, and dark cedars in the lower valleys; giving way above the tree line to black basalt scree, sulfur crusts, and smoking vents.
- **Materials:** Columnar black basalt, obsidian (dragonglass) veins, pumice, ochre sulfur deposits, red-gold and copper ore seams.
- **Architectural Style:** *Pre-Freehold:* Minimal pastoral dry-stone walls; *Early Freehold:* First dark fused-stone terraces cutting into the lower calderas.
- **Dominant Colors:** Obsidian black (`#141416`), volcanic ash grey (`#3E4044`), lush volcanic pasture emerald (`#2D5A36`), molten magma orange-gold (`#E85D1A`), Summer Sea deep cerulean (`#1B4D6E`).
- **Lighting Conditions:** Dramatic dual-source lighting—brilliant warm southern sun piercing towering plumes of ash and steam by day; haunting under-lit crimson magma glow reflecting off clouds at dusk and night.
- **Cultural Characteristics:** Sacred, perilous homeland of the early Valyrians; source of both agricultural bounty and subterranean mineral/dragon power.
- **Typical Inhabitants:** Early Valyrian pastoralists in the valleys; wild dragons in upper volcanic caverns; wingless firewyrms in deep magma tubes.
- **Distinctive Landmarks:** The central crescent of three tallest smoking peaks of the Fourteen Flames overlooking a winding emerald valley to the Summer Sea.

## 2. `LOC-VAL-002` — PASTORAL FOOTHILLS & VALYRIAN SHEEPHERDER SETTLEMENT
- **Location Name & ID:** `LOC-VAL-002` — Early Valyrian Pastoral Foothills & Basalt Homesteads
- **Historical Period:** Pre-Dragon Pastoral Era, ca. 5,000+ BC
- **Geography:** Terraced green highland meadows nestled at the base of dark hexagonal basalt cliffs, fed by warm geothermal mineral streams.
- **Climate:** Mild, misty mornings with warm geothermal steam rising from hot springs; golden sunlit afternoons.
- **Vegetation:** Thick mineral-rich pasture grass, wild mountain herbs, twisted olive and dark cypress trees.
- **Materials:** Unmortared stacked black volcanic basalt boulders, weathered cedar timber rafters, slate-shingle roofs, woven wattle sheep hurdles.
- **Architectural Style:** Humble, low-slung circular and rectangular dry-stone pastoral dwellings and circular stone sheepfolds hugging the mountain contours—utterly devoid of grand imperial masonry.
- **Dominant Colors:** Mossy green, wet basalt charcoal, undyed cream sheep's wool, warm hearth-amber, pale mist grey.
- **Lighting Conditions:** Soft golden morning rays slicing through geothermal valley mist, with the distant smoking cones of the Fourteen Flames towering on the horizon.
- **Cultural Characteristics:** Quiet, communal, isolated clan life centered on wool, cheese, bronze/bone craft, and caution toward the high peaks.
- **Typical Inhabitants:** Silver-haired, violet-eyed early Valyrian families (`CHAR-VAL-001`, `CHAR-VAL-002` Stage A) and large flocks of long-wooled mountain sheep.
- **Distinctive Landmarks:** Circular basalt sheep-pens beside a steaming mineral stream with the Fourteen Flames looming directly behind.

## 3. `LOC-VAL-003` — UPPER VOLCANIC CAVERNS & WILD DRAGON LAIRS
- **Location Name & ID:** `LOC-VAL-003` — The High Caldera Caverns of the Fourteen Flames
- **Historical Period:** Era of Dragon Discovery, ca. 5,000+ BC
- **Geography:** Jagged upper flanks of the Fourteen Flames at 6,000+ feet—natural lava tubes, cathedral-sized basalt caverns, and sulfur-rimmed vents opening onto sheer volcanic precipices.
- **Climate:** Scorching geothermal heat, dry sulfurous updrafts, drifting ash flakes.
- **Vegetation:** Barren—zero plant life; only crystalline yellow sulfur blooms and glassy obsidian formations.
- **Materials:** Natural hexagonal basalt columns, vitrified glass-smooth magma stone melted by centuries of dragonflame, charred sheep and beast bones, glowing lava fissures.
- **Architectural Style:** Purely geological—nature's own terrifying cathedrals of stone, with natural rock ledges polished smooth by coiled dragon scales.
- **Dominant Colors:** Pitch basalt black, sulfur ochre, ember crimson, smoky slate, molten gold.
- **Lighting Conditions:** High-contrast chiaroscuro—cool blue-grey daylight at the cavern mouth clashing with fierce orange-red magma light pulsing from deep fissures below.
- **Cultural Characteristics:** Forbidden threshold where mortal shepherds first confronted living fire.
- **Typical Inhabitants:** Wild dragons (`DRG-VAL-001`, `DRG-VAL-002` pre-binding), hatchlings, and deep-burrowing firewyrms (`CRT-VAL-001`).
- **Distinctive Landmarks:** A colossal vaulted cavern mouth framed by natural hexagonal basalt pillars overlooking a sea of volcanic clouds.

## 4. `LOC-VAL-004` — EARLY CITADEL OF THE VALYRIAN FREEHOLD (PROTO-METROPOLIS)
- **Location Name & ID:** `LOC-VAL-004` — Rising Valyria: The Early Fused-Stone Freehold City
- **Historical Period:** Founding & Early Ascendance of the Valyrian Freehold, ca. 5,000 BC
- **Geography:** Built directly astride dramatic basalt ridges and channeled lava rivers between the lower peaks of the Fourteen Flames, stepping down toward natural deep-water harbors.
- **Climate:** Warm, luminous, dramatic skies streaked with volcanic haze and drifting embers.
- **Vegetation:** Enclosed terraced hanging gardens of cypress, pomegranate, and blood-red blossoms contrasting against black stone.
- **Materials:** **Seamless dragonflame-fused black stone** (glossy-matte obsidian-like monolithic masonry with **zero mortar lines or chisel marks**), hammered red-gold, burnished bronze, carnelian, and glowing lava channels.
- **Architectural Style:** Distinctively **non-European**, ancient volcanic-imperial grandeur: soaring, organically twisted black stone dragon-towers opening at the crown into sky-roosts; sweeping monolithic bridges spanning controlled canals of molten rock; colossal carved black-stone **Valyrian sphinxes** (dragon body, human face) flanking grand processional avenues; early construction zones showing pyromancers and dragons actively fusing new stone walls!
- **Dominant Colors:** Glossy fused obsidian black (`#101114`), molten lava gold-crimson (`#EA4C15`), burnished bronze (`#9E6B3B`), Valyrian crimson banners (`#7A1218`), twilight violet haze.
- **Lighting Conditions:** Epic volumetric sunlight filtering through high volcanic smoke by day; warm, awe-inspiring under-glow from engineered lava canals and bronze fire-braziers at dusk.
- **Cultural Characteristics:** Aristocratic republic of the Forty Dragonlord Houses—wealthy, arcane, proud, sorcerous, terrifyingly advanced.
- **Typical Inhabitants:** Early Dragonlords (`CHAR-VAL-002` Stage C), Fire-Mages (`CHAR-VAL-003`), freeholders, artisans, and roosting dragons overhead.
- **Distinctive Landmarks:** The **First Council Hall of the Freeholders** (open-colonnaded fused-black-stone amphitheater ringed by forty bronze fire-tripods) and the **Rising First Dragon-Towers** flanked by twin Valyrian sphinxes.

## 5. `LOC-VAL-005` — SUBTERRANEAN MINES OF THE FOURTEEN FLAMES
- **Location Name & ID:** `LOC-VAL-005` — The Deep Ore Shafts Beneath the Fourteen Flames
- **Historical Period:** Early Freehold Mining Expansion, ca. 5,000 BC
- **Geography:** Labyrinthine subterranean caverns and hand-driven ore tunnels honeycombing the fiery roots of the Fourteen Flames.
- **Climate:** Suffocating, blistering heat, scalding steam jets, choking sulfurous air.
- **Vegetation:** None.
- **Materials:** Raw jagged volcanic rock laced with glittering veins of native gold, silver, red copper, and iron; heavy charred timber shoring beams; iron chains and bronze ore-baskets.
- **Architectural Style:** Brutal extractive pit-engineering—rope-and-bronze windlasses spanning yawning magma chasms, narrow rock-cut ledges, jagged boreholes left by burrowing firewyrms.
- **Dominant Colors:** Soot black, sweat-gleaming ochre, glowing magma red, metallic gold/copper glints, choking grey-yellow steam.
- **Lighting Conditions:** Pitch subterranean darkness pierced only by guttering oil lamps, glowing rock fissures, and sudden bursts of scalding white steam.
- **Cultural Characteristics:** The grim, hidden engine of Valyrian wealth—where human bondage took root beneath the Freehold's shining towers (*AFFC* Arya II).
- **Typical Inhabitants:** Exhausted soot-covered captive miners, bronze-armored early Valyrian overseers, and lurking subterranean **firewyrms** (`CRT-VAL-001`).
- **Distinctive Landmarks:** A vast vertical mining chasm spanned by timber-and-chain winches directly above a dull-glowing magma crust.

## 6. `LOC-VAL-006` — THE FIRST VALYRIAN DRAGONROAD
- **Location Name & ID:** `LOC-VAL-006` — Early Fused-Stone Valyrian Dragonroad
- **Historical Period:** Early Freehold Consolidation, ca. 5,000 BC
- **Geography:** Cutting arrow-straight across the rolling green valleys and rocky passes of the Valyrian peninsula toward the coast.
- **Materials & Architecture:** Canon-exact (*ADWD* Tyrion II): a broad ribbon of **seamless fused grey-black stone**, smooth and unjointed, raised half a foot above the surrounding soil, impervious to weeds, frost, or erosion, wide enough for three wagons abreast.
- **Dominant Colors:** Matte charcoal stone cutting through emerald grass and golden coastal sunlight.

## 7. `LOC-ESS-001` — OLD GHIS (IMPERIAL CAPITAL OF THE GHISCARI EMPIRE)
- **Location Name & ID:** `LOC-ESS-001` — Old Ghis, Capital on the Gulf of Grief
- **Historical Period:** Peak Pre-Valyrian Ghiscari Dominance, pre-5,000 BC
- **Geography:** Sun-baked arid coastal plain on the eastern shore of the Gulf of Grief, overlooking a crowded imperial harbor of bronze-rammed galleys.
- **Climate:** Hot, dry, sun-drenched, dusty golden haze off the coastal desert.
- **Vegetation:** Date palms, cedars, irrigated palace gardens of pomegranate and cypress contrasting with dry ochre hills.
- **Materials:** Fired yellow, red, and glazed polychrome **mud-brick** (*ASOS* Daenerys III explicitly states Old Ghis had *brick* walls and stepped pyramids!), cast bronze, gilded reliefs, limestone paving.
- **Architectural Style:** Monumental ancient Near-Eastern / Mesopotamian-inspired grandeur (completely distinct from Valyria's fused black stone!): colossal **stepped brick pyramids** rising hundreds of feet with lush hanging terraces, thick crenellated brick walls, wide parade avenues, and towering bronze statues of the **Harpy of Old Ghis clutching a thunderbolt in her talons**.
- **Dominant Colors:** Sun-baked ochre brick (`#C88A4B`), terracotta red (`#9E3B24`), verdigris & burnished bronze (`#8A6230`), glazed lapis blue tiles (`#1F4E79`), blazing desert gold.
- **Lighting Conditions:** Harsh, brilliant golden Mediterranean/desert sunlight casting sharp geometric shadows across stepped brick facades.
- **Cultural Characteristics:** Rigid, ancient slave-owning military empire; proud patricians in fringed *tokars*, endless columns of chained captives, and iron-disciplined **Lockstep Legions** marching to great bronze drums.
- **Typical Inhabitants:** Grazdan the Great (`CHAR-GHI-001`), *tokar*-clad Ghiscari patricians, Lockstep Legionaries (tall shields, three spears of graduated lengths, bronze/leather scale, plumed helms), enslaved laborers.
- **Distinctive Landmarks:** The **Great Brick Pyramid of Old Ghis** crowned by a 100-foot cast-bronze **Harpy clutching a thunderbolt**, overlooking the Grand Parade Plaza of the Lockstep Legions.

## 8. `LOC-ESS-002` — ANCIENT RIVER SETTLEMENTS OF THE MOTHER RHOYNE
- **Location Name & ID:** `LOC-ESS-002` — Early Rhoynar River-Towns on the Mother Rhoyne
- **Historical Period:** Pre-Valyrian Antiquity, ca. 5,000+ BC
- **Geography:** Immense, tranquil silver-green expanse of the **River Rhoyne**—so wide its far bank vanishes into golden mist—lined with lush willow banks, islands, and marble-and-sandstone docks.
- **Climate:** Warm, gentle, humid river breeze, golden afternoon light.
- **Materials & Architecture:** Carved pale rose-and-cream river sandstone, graceful arched bridges, open water-pavilions, shallow-draft gilded river skiffs, and early riverside **iron-smelting bloomeries** glowing beside waterwheels.
- **Dominant Colors:** River jade-silver, warm rose sandstone, willow green, glowing forge-orange.
- **Typical Inhabitants:** Olive-skinned, dark-haired Rhoynar artisans, fishers, musicians, and early iron-smiths; giant river turtles basking near shrine steps.

## 9. `LOC-ESS-003` — ASSHAI-BY-THE-SHADOW
- **Location Name & ID:** `LOC-ESS-003` — Asshai-by-the-Shadow & The River Ash
- **Historical Period:** Timeless Antiquity (Pre-5,000 BC – Present)
- **Geography:** Far southeastern tip of Essos where the narrow, toxic black **River Ash** emerges from the towering **Mountains of the Morn / Shadow Lands** into the Jade Gates.
- **Climate:** Oppressive, perpetual twilight gloom even at midday; pallid mist, zero children, zero birds or beasts.
- **Materials & Architecture:** Canon-exact (*TWOIAF*): colossal cyclopean palaces, temples, walls, and harbor piers carved entirely of **greasy, light-drinking black stone** that feels slick to the touch and absorbs torchlight rather than reflecting it (distinct from Valyria's glossy heat-fused stone—Asshai's stone is dull, greasy, ancient, and light-devouring!).
- **Dominant Colors:** Abyssal greasy black (`#0A0B0D`), sickly pale ghostly green-grey mist (`#5B6B65`), dim blood-red shadowbinder lanterns (`#7A1818`).
- **Typical Inhabitants:** Masked shadowbinders (`CHAR-ASH-001`), silent robed figures gliding on narrow poles-boats across the black waters of the Ash.
