# H. UNRESOLVED LORE & DISPUTED HISTORY DATABASE
**Database Module:** `H_UNRESOLVED_LORE_DATABASE`  
**Canon Rule (Section 2 & Section 17.12):** Preserve George R. R. Martin's intentional historical ambiguities. Never turn theories into established facts. Always frame these entries with explicit historiographical qualifiers (*"According to surviving accounts..."*, *"Some scholars believe..."*, *"In Asshai, tales are told..."*, *"The truth remains uncertain..."*).
