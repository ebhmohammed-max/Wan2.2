## 2. Creatures

### The Others
✅ Tall, gaunt and hard as old bones, with milk-pale flesh. Their eyes are deeper and bluer than any human eyes, "a blue that burned like ice". Their armor changes color as they move, like moonlight on water. Thin crystal swords glow faintly blue and shatter steel. Intense cold comes with them. (AGOT Prologue)
- They are **not** the show's horned Night King and not corpse-like.
- No leader design is canon. Do not invent a "Night King".

### Wights
✅ The risen dead, with pale flesh, **black hands** and glowing blue eyes.

### Dragons
◻ **Two legs plus two wings**; the wings are the forelimbs (GRRM, tier 3).

| Dragon | Colors |
|---|---|
| **Drogon** | Black with scarlet markings |
| **Rhaegal** | Green and bronze |
| **Viserion** | Cream and gold |
| **Balerion** (history) | Black, colossal |

They grow throughout their lives. In ADWD the three are still young, about horse-to-small-ship scale. Always state the scale relative to people.

## 3. Objects and heraldry
- **Iron Throne:** ✅ Huge, hulking, black, asymmetric and ugly. It is made from a thousand or more half-melted, twisted swords hammered together by blacksmiths. Steep iron steps lead up to a high seat about 10 ft above the court. It "towers over the room like a great beast". Reclining is impossible. (GRRM 2013 and 2014, Simonetti art = reference for *concept* only.)
  - **NOT** the show's compact chair.
- **Valyrian steel:** dark, smoky, rippled steel.
  - *Ice*: a greatsword as wide as a man's hand.
  - *Longclaw*: a bastard sword with a pale-stone wolf's-head pommel with garnet eyes.
  - *Oathkeeper* and *Widow's Wail*: red-and-black ripples.
- **Weirwoods:** bone-white bark and red five-pointed leaves like bloody hands. Heart trees have carved faces weeping red sap.
- **Orders and dress:**
  - Kingsguard: white enamelled scale armor and white cloaks.
  - Night's Watch: all black, worn and roughspun.
  - Maesters: grey robes and chain collars of mixed metals.
  - The Hand of the King wears a hand-shaped pin.
- **Heraldry is allowed** (described as images on banners and shields). **Never letters or words** on anything.

## 5. Prompt rules (Westeros additions to the DUN pipeline rules)
- Always add: "book-accurate, NOT the HBO TV design, original faces, not resembling any actor".
- Medieval technology only: horses, sail and oar, plate and mail, torches and candles. **NO** gunpowder, glass windows outside the wealthy, or modern faces, hairstyles or makeup.
- Keep the existing pipeline rules: no text or letters, letterbox auto-crop, and portrait anchors in batch 1 reused as references.
