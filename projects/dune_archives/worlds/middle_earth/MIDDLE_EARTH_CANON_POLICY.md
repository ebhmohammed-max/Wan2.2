# MIDDLE-EARTH CANON POLICY v1.0 (Mythic Archives, 2026-11-25)

Applies to every Middle-earth (LOTR###) episode. It mirrors `worlds/westeros/WESTEROS_CANON_POLICY.md`; where the two differ for Middle-earth, this file wins.

## 1. Source hierarchy (higher always wins)

| Tier | Source | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | *The Lord of the Rings* (incl. Appendices), *The Hobbit* | `[B]` |
| 2 | *The Silmarillion* (Valaquenta, Quenta, Akallabêth, Of the Rings of Power) | `[B]` |
| 3 | *Unfinished Tales*, *The Children of Húrin*, *Beren and Lúthien*, *The Fall of Gondolin* | `[B]` |
| 4 | *The History of Middle-earth*, *Nature of Middle-earth*, *Parma Eldalamberon* / *Vinyar Tengwar* (late notes) | `[N]`: say "in later notes" / "in notes published long after the books" |
| 5 | The author's published *Letters* | `[L]`: use as "the legends say" or the books' position, never a named quote |
| 6 | Reference works (Tolkien Gateway, Encyclopedia of Arda), articles | fact-check aid only |
| 7 | Films (Jackson) and *The Rings of Power* | `[S]`: named openly as "the films" / "the show" and always labelled |

## 2. Narration rules (director's orders)

- **Never name the author** (director, 2026-11-25, carried over from GoT). No "Tolkien", "J.R.R.", "the Professor" in narration, graphics, chapter titles or YouTube copy. Book facts are **"in the books"**, or the specific book ("in *The Silmarillion*") where that helps.
- Late-notes material (e.g. the name *Mairon*) is labelled "in later notes" / "notes published long after the books", never presented as main-text canon.
- Show and film inventions (e.g. *Halbrand*, the giant flaming Eye on the tower) are named and labelled as the show's or the films'.
- Theories are framed as theories ("most readers believe…").

## 3. Fact-check gate (every script, before VO)

Every claim in the fact sheet gets a tier tag and a ✅ once it has been checked against a quoted passage. Quotes are checked word for word; paraphrase when unsure.

## 4. Content safety

- TTS blocks graphic violence. Narrate deaths and torment plainly and briefly (no blood, no "teeth", no gore).
- Image moderation: no gore, no human sacrifice shown (use an empty altar, smoke, or a temple exterior), no mob with improvised weapons.

## 4b. Likeness rule (director's order 2026-11-25, carried over from GoT)

- **No actor or celebrity faces.** Nothing resembling Elijah Wood, Ian McKellen, Viggo Mortensen, Cate Blanchett, Hugo Weaving, Andy Serkis, Charlie Vickers, Morfydd Clark, Charles Edwards, Robert Aramayo, etc.
- No recreation of film or show production design that identifies a specific production: the Jackson Barad-dûr spire with a flaming Eye, the film Sauron armour, the *Rings of Power* Halbrand / Annatar looks.
- Characters are drawn from the **book descriptions** (`MIDDLE_EARTH_BOOK_LOOKS.md`). Show-only characters (Halbrand) appear only as silhouettes or from behind.
- Face QC on every still with a face (Visual Style Bible §8). Redo anything that drifts towards an actor.
