# THE DUNE ARCHIVES — Storytelling & Hook Framework (v1.0 — 2026-11-23)

Companion to `HOOK_LIBRARY.md`/`.csv`, which holds 1,065 draft hooks (5 per topic × 213 topics).

## 1. The five hook types (every topic has one of each)

| ID | Type | Placement | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | Question | 0:00–0:10 | Opens a curiosity gap the episode closes |
| H2 | Revelation | 0:10–0:30 | Sets up a contradiction or surprising canonical fact |
| H3 | Cold open (visual + VO) | First shot | Image first, question in VO |
| H4 | Stakes | Re-hook at ~25% | Why it matters to the saga |
| H5 | Archive signature | After the cold open | Channel brand plus continuity transparency |

## 2. Episode spine ("The Archive Structure")

1. **Cold open (0:00–0:45).** H3 plus H2. One striking reconstruction.
2. **Archive card (0:45–1:00).** H5. Logo sting, era tag and source labels.
3. **Context (≈10%).** Where and when we are, with a map (`map_animator.py`).
4. **Rising record (≈40%).** Chronological events, each claim labelled.
5. **Re-hook (≈25% mark).** H4 stakes.
6. **The fracture (≈20%).** Where continuities disagree: book vs EU vs film.
7. **Consequence (≈15%).** What changed in the saga.
8. **Coda (≈5%).** Herbert's theme, the next-episode tease, and the reconstruction disclaimer.

EPIC episodes use chapters (5–9 chapters, each 5–10 minutes) built on the same spine.

## 3. Narration voice

- Calm, authoritative, archival: "The records show…", "In Frank Herbert's account…", "The expanded novels tell a different story…".
- Present tense for reconstruction sequences; past tense for analysis.
- Lines are short (≤ 20 words) for VO pacing, at roughly 140–150 words per minute.
- Signposted spoiler levels: `SPOILERS: DUNE MESSIAH`.

## 4. Hook QC (all must pass before use)

- [ ] The hook is true under the topic's continuity label.
- [ ] The episode fully pays off the hook (no false promise).
- [ ] No sensational words that misrepresent the content.
- [ ] No phrasing copied from competitor titles or scripts.
- [ ] Any number in the hook is verified against an edition/page.
- [ ] No actor names in hooks or titles.

## 5. Retention devices

- Open loops: pose a question in the first act and pay it off in the fracture section.
- **Pattern interrupts** every 60–90 s: map beat, artifact insert, era card or canon-label card.
- **Visual escalation:** the biggest reconstruction is held back for the consequence section.
- **Chapter titles** on screen in EPIC episodes.
