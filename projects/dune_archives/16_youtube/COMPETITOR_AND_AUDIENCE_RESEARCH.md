## 2. Observed patterns (what works)

1. **Timeline and "complete history" mega-videos** pull the biggest numbers. Quinn's 34,000-year timeline video has 3M+ views. → Our EPIC tier exists for this, with a visual moat.
2. **Release-window spikes:** trailer analysis gets hundreds of thousands of views within weeks. → *Dune: Part Three* opens **18 Dec 2026**. Messiah primers are most valuable **before** release; book-vs-film topics are most valuable in the week **after**.
3. **Late-book demand is real** (Golden Path and Leto II videos in Oct–Nov 2026) and **under-served visually**. Leto II has never appeared on film.
4. **"What the films changed" and "myths fans get wrong"** are perennial and low-risk, provided continuity labels are applied.
5. **Sleep and long-watch formats** show appetite for 40 min+ narration. Our EPIC tier can serve the same viewers with higher energy plus chapter markers.

## 3. Audience profile (inferred, not measured)

- **Segment A — Film-first newcomers** (largest). They know the Villeneuve films and want "what happens next?" ahead of Part Three. They need spoiler-labelled, accessible explanations.
- **Segment B — Book readers.** They want canon precision, Herbert's intent, and late-book eras (God Emperor, Heretics). They punish errors and continuity mixing.
- **Segment C — Lore and worldbuilding fans** (Templin-style). They want factions, technology and politics.
- **Segment D — Long-watch / ambient viewers.** They want EPIC narration, chapters and a calm pace.

## 4. Strategic positioning

- **Moat:** a *reconstruction* aesthetic with a strict canon-label discipline. No competitor found combines (a) original photoreal 3D reconstruction, (b) on-screen continuity labels and (c) single-universe depth.
- **Title discipline:** curiosity without deception. No "SHOCKING", no false claims and no fake leaks.
- **Thumbnail discipline:** one original character or object plus a 2–3 word label. No actor faces and no film stills. Every thumbnail needs creative director approval.
- **Cadence (proposal):** 1 STANDARD every 1–2 weeks. MINI pieces are cut from STANDARD research (Shorts and 16:9 minis). 1 EPIC per quarter.
