## Strongest first documentary → **#1 DUN003, *Dune Messiah Explained Before Part Three***

- **Timing.** The audience peak is predictable and close: the weeks before 18 Dec 2026. Film-first viewers will search "what happens in Dune Messiah" and "what happens in Part Three".
- **Scope.** STANDARD length is achievable in about three weeks: script, roughly 80–110 unique stills in 8–11 batches, CPU motion and the edit.
- **Canon safety.** The topic is pure ORIGINAL canon (one book). Film comparisons are deferred to a post-release follow-up.
- **Series engine.** It feeds directly into Hayt (#3), Golden Path (#4) and the Leto II epic (#9).
- **Risk.** Competition will be heavy (Quinn's Ideas and others). Our differentiation is reconstruction visuals plus canon labels. Avoid predicting the film's plot; frame it as "what the book says".

**Alternative first pick (if you prefer evergreen over timely):** DUN001, the Butlerian Jihad. It is the channel's identity piece but carries the biggest image budget (EPIC: about 250–400 unique stills).
