## House Atreides

1. **Name:** House Atreides
2. **Status:** Great House (Landsraad) → Imperial House under Paul and Leto II
3. **Homeworld:** Caladan (ocean world)
4. **Seat:** Castle Caladan; later Arrakeen (Arrakis)
5. **Heraldry:** Red hawk crest (ORIGINAL)
6. **Colours Visual:** Green and black (ORIGINAL references to 'green and black Atreides'); weathered naval aesthetic, stone and sea [VERIFY colour pairing in text]
7. **Origin:** Claims descent from the House of Atreus (ORIGINAL: Paul's ancestry is linked to Agamemnon in Dune). EU: Vorian Atreides.
8. **Key Members:** Leto I, Paul, Alia, Leto II, Ghanima; later Siona, Moneo, Miles Teg, Odrade (bloodline)
9. **Power Base:** Popularity in the Landsraad; 'the Atreides form' of loyal, highly trained troops
10. **Military:** Small but elite army rivalling Sardaukar (the Emperor's fear); Fremen after 10191
11. **Economy:** Pundi rice (Caladan); later spice monopoly via Arrakis
12. **Culture:** Honour, loyalty, justice; father-to-son duty; bullfighting heritage (EU detail) [VERIFY]
13. **Allies:** Fremen; Gurney's smugglers; (EU) House Vernius, Ecaz
14. **Rivals:** House Harkonnen (ancient feud); House Corrino
15. **Fate:** Becomes the Imperial line; Leto II's breeding seeds the Atreides bloodline across the Scattering
16. **Continuity Notes:** Villeneuve films emphasise Atreides heraldry in bullfight imagery; 1984 film colours differ. Never lift film costume designs.

## House Harkonnen

1. **Name:** House Harkonnen
2. **Status:** Great House; Siridar-Barons; stewards of Arrakis before 10191
3. **Homeworld:** Giedi Prime (later renamed Gammu)
4. **Seat:** Harko City / Barony on Giedi Prime
5. **Heraldry:** Blue griffin (ORIGINAL)
6. **Colours Visual:** Blue and black; industrial, oily, brutal geometry [Films: monochrome black-sun aesthetic = FILM ADAPTATION]
7. **Origin:** Ancient feud with Atreides; EU: Abulurd branded coward after Corrin (88 BG)
8. **Key Members:** Baron Vladimir, Feyd-Rautha, Glossu Rabban, Piter de Vries (servant); EU/TV: Valya & Tula, Xavier, Abulurd
9. **Power Base:** CHOAM directorship wealth; spice profits from Arrakis; cruelty as policy
10. **Military:** Harkonnen troops plus covert Sardaukar support in 10191
11. **Economy:** Spice profits; Giedi Prime industry; whale fur (Lankiveil, EU detail)
12. **Culture:** Cruelty, decadence, slavery, gladiatorial games, poisons
13. **Allies:** Emperor Shaddam IV (secretly, 10191)
14. **Rivals:** House Atreides
15. **Fate:** Destroyed as a power after Paul's victory; secret blood survives in Jessica → Paul → all Atreides
16. **Continuity Notes:** Black-sun and albino Giedi Prime are Part Two inventions. The secret Harkonnen-Atreides blood is ORIGINAL.
