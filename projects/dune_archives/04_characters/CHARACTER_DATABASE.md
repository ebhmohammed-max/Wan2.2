- **Aliases:** Muad'Dib; Usul; Kwisatz Haderach; the Preacher (later)
- **Affiliation:** House Atreides → Fremen → Emperor

- **Era:** 10175–10219 AG
- **First Appearance:** Dune (1965)
- **Origin Label:** ORIGINAL FRANK HERBERT CANON
- **Role:** Heir who becomes messiah-emperor; his jihad and prescience define the saga.
- **Relationships:** Son of Leto I & Jessica; partner of Chani; husband (in name) of Irulan; father of Leto II & Ghanima; brother of Alia
- **Abilities:** Bene Gesserit training, Mentat training, prescience, Fremen fighting, worm riding
- **Book Fate:** Blinded (Messiah), walks into desert; returns as the blind Preacher; killed in Arrakeen (Children of Dune)
- **Adaptation Notes:** 2021/2024 films: Chani rejects his jihad (ADAPTATION-SPECIFIC). 1984 film: makes it rain, 'weirding modules'. Part Three (2026) adapts Messiah.
- **Visual Brief:** Slight, dark-haired, grey-green eyes turning full blue-in-blue (spice); posture of trained stillness; Fremen stillsuit with Atreides hawk motif later replaced by Imperial black-and-green
- **Doc Potential:** 10
- **Verify:** Exact age at Messiah; wording of the Preacher's death
