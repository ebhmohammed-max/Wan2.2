# THE DUNE ARCHIVES — Character Continuity Sheets (TEXT v1.0 — 2026-11-23)

**Purpose:** lock an ORIGINAL face and silhouette for each recurring character so that every image batch stays consistent.

**Rules:**
- These are text reference sheets only. No images have been generated. Reference images will be made only after the creative director approves the topic and image quantity, and each character design will need the director's approval.
- Designs derive from the novels' textual descriptions plus original channel choices. They are **not based on any actor** who has played these roles.
- Prompts must never name actors or films.
- Generated art is never described as official.

**Sheet fields:**
- ID
- Canon basis (what the text says)
- Channel design choices (marked ORIGINAL DESIGN)
- Locked traits (never change)
- Variable traits (change by era)
- Era variants
- Prompt anchor (paste into prompts)
- Avoid list

---

## CS-PAUL — Paul Atreides

- **Canon basis (ORIGINAL):** Atreides heir; described with dark hair and his grandfather's features. Spice turns his eyes blue-in-blue on Arrakis. `[VERIFY exact physical description wording in Dune Book I]`
- **ORIGINAL DESIGN:**
  - Face: narrow oval, high forehead and a pronounced straight nose bridge, lightly freckled across the nose.
  - Brows: thick, low-set dark brows.
  - Hair: black-brown with a slight wave, cut short at the sides.
  - Build: lean, wiry, below-average height in youth.
- **Locked:** brow shape, nose bridge, small scar above the left eyebrow (ORIGINAL DESIGN, from training) and the freckle pattern.
- **Variants:**
  - `CS-PAUL-A`, Caladan, age 15: grey-green eyes, Atreides house tunic.
  - `CS-PAUL-B`, Fremen Usul, 16–17: tanned skin, blue-tinged eyes, stillsuit, hair bound.
  - `CS-PAUL-C`, Emperor, ~27 (Messiah): blue-in-blue eyes, close beard, black imperial robe with a muted hawk motif.
  - `CS-PAUL-D`, blind Emperor: scarred, burned eye sockets with prosthetic-free dark lenses (ORIGINAL DESIGN). Render tastefully, never graphic.
  - `CS-PAUL-E`, the Preacher: gaunt and long-haired, rough desert robe, led by a guide.
- **Prompt anchor:** "young man with a narrow oval face, high forehead, straight pronounced nose bridge, light freckles across the nose, thick low dark brows, short wavy black-brown hair, small pale scar above left eyebrow, wiry build"
- **Avoid:** any resemblance to a known performer; overly glamorous styling; modern haircuts.

## CS-JESSICA — Lady Jessica

- **Canon basis:** Bene Gesserit; bronze/auburn hair and green eyes in the text `[VERIFY]`.
- **ORIGINAL DESIGN:**
  - Face: long heart-shaped face with wide-set eyes.
  - Features: small mole at the right jaw-line; poised, unmoving expression.
  - Build: tall.
- **Locked:** jaw mole, eye spacing, hair colour.
- **Variants:**

  - `-B` Bene Gesserit black aba.
  - `-C` Fremen Reverend Mother: aba with water rings.
  - `-D` Older Jessica (Children of Dune): silver streaks.
- **Prompt anchor:** "tall woman, long heart-shaped face, wide-set green eyes, bronze-auburn hair, small mole on right jawline, composed motionless expression"
- **Avoid:** facial tattoos (a film choice); actor resemblance.

## CS-BARON — Baron Vladimir Harkonnen

- **Canon basis:** grossly obese and supported by suspensors.
- **ORIGINAL DESIGN:**
  - Face: enormous pale mass with jowls and tiny bright black eyes; hairless scalp with liver spots.
  - Hands: rings on every finger.
  - Clothing: layered black-and-blue silks.
- **Locked:** eye colour, rings, suspensor belt glow (faint amber).
