# THE DUNE ARCHIVES — Canon & Sourcing Policy (v1.0 — 2026-11-23)

This document governs every script, caption, map, thumbnail and image prompt the channel produces.

## 1. Source hierarchy (highest authority first)

1. **Frank Herbert's six novels**: *Dune* (1965, with its appendices and Terminology of the Imperium), *Dune Messiah* (1969), *Children of Dune* (1976), *God Emperor of Dune* (1981), *Heretics of Dune* (1984) and *Chapterhouse: Dune* (1985).
2. **Expanded-universe novels** by Brian Herbert & Kevin J. Anderson: the Prelude, Legends, Heroes/interquels, Hunters/Sandworms, Great Schools and Caladan series, and *Princess of Dune*.
3. **Film and TV adaptations**: Lynch 1984; Sci-Fi Channel 2000/2003; Villeneuve 2021/2024/2026; *Dune: Prophecy* 2024–.
4. **Creator interviews**, cited only from a verifiable transcript or recording.
5. **Secondary research**: encyclopedias, wikis and fan timelines. These are used for leads only and are never the sole source for an on-screen claim.

## 2. Mandatory continuity labels

Every claim in a research note or script carries one of these labels:

| Label | Use when |
|---|---|
| ORIGINAL FRANK HERBERT CANON | The claim is stated in the six novels or their appendices |
| EXPANDED DUNE UNIVERSE | The claim comes only from BH/KJA novels |
| FILM ADAPTATION | The claim is shown in a feature film |
| TELEVISION ADAPTATION | The claim is shown in a TV series or miniseries |
| SHARED OR OVERLAPPING CONTINUITY | Multiple sources agree on the claim (state which ones) |
| ADAPTATION-SPECIFIC INTERPRETATION | An adaptation deliberately departs from the books (e.g. Chani leaving in *Part Two*, Alia unborn, Paul killing the Baron, weirding modules, "the spice must flow") |
| FAN THEORY | A community interpretation, a what-if, or the non-canon *Dune Encyclopedia* |
| CREATIVE RECONSTRUCTION | The channel's own visual or geographic inference |

**Never merge continuities.** When sources differ, the script presents them side by side and names each one.

## 3. On-screen disclosure

- The lower-third canon tag appears for **every** disputed claim, for example: `SOURCE: ORIGINAL NOVELS` or `SOURCE: EXPANDED UNIVERSE`.
- Reconstructed visuals carry the end-card disclaimer: *"Visual reconstructions are original artistic interpretations, not official artwork, and do not depict any actor."*
- Interstellar maps always carry: *"Schematic reconstruction — relative positions are artistic."*

## 4. Quotations

- Quote only from a specific checked edition, logged in `25_RESEARCH_SOURCES_AND_CITATIONS` with page or chapter.
- Never invent a quotation, and never paraphrase inside quotation marks.
- Known misattributions to avoid:
  - "The spice must flow" is from the 1984 film, not the novel.
  - "He who controls the spice controls the universe" is film dialogue.
- Herbert interview quotes require a transcript or recording reference.

## 5. Figures and dates

- Every number spoken on screen must be marked `[VERIFIED: edition/page]` in the script. Examples: casualties of Muad'Dib's jihad, worm lengths, reign lengths, AG dates.
- Use relative anchors on screen ("twelve years after Paul's ascension") when absolute AG years are computed rather than stated.
- Gregorian conversions are FAN THEORY and never appear as fact.

## 6. Likeness and rights

- Character designs are original. **No actor likenesses.** Prompts never name actors, films or costume designers.
- No film frames, film costume replicas, film ornithopter/ship designs, or film logos/typography.
- No competitor scripts, titles or thumbnail compositions.
- Fair use and monetisation are never promised. Any rights question goes to the creative director.

## 7. Disputed-topic register (initial)

| Dispute | Positions | Handling |
|---|---|---|
| Butlerian Jihad's nature | Herbert: human revolt, no robot war. *Dune Encyclopedia*: Jehanne Butler (non-canon). EU: Serena Butler vs Omnius/Erasmus | "Three versions" segment, each labelled |
| *Hunters/Sandworms* as the "true" ending | EU authors cite Frank Herbert's Dune 7 notes; many readers dispute this | Present the claim as the EU authors' statement |
| Paul: hero vs warning | Herbert interviews and *Messiah* text vs popular reception | Quote only verified sources |
| Alia, Chani and the Baron's death in the Villeneuve films | Book vs film | ADAPTATION-SPECIFIC label |
| Prophecy vs *Sisterhood of Dune* | TV reworks characters and events | Separate rows; never align dates |
| AG dating of late eras | Secondary timelines disagree | Relative anchors on screen |
