# THE DUNE ARCHIVES — Master Historical Timeline (v1.0 — 2026-11-23)

> **Continuity labels are mandatory.** ORIGINAL FRANK HERBERT CANON · EXPANDED DUNE UNIVERSE · FILM ADAPTATION · TELEVISION ADAPTATION · SHARED OR OVERLAPPING CONTINUITY · ADAPTATION-SPECIFIC INTERPRETATION · FAN THEORY · CREATIVE RECONSTRUCTION. Never merge continuities. `[VERIFY]` = check against the printed text before scripting.

Calendar: **BG** = Before Guild, **AG** = After Guild. Gregorian conversions (e.g. '1 AG ≈ 13360 AD') are **FAN THEORY / CREATIVE RECONSTRUCTION** and must never appear on screen as fact.

Confidence: **HIGH** = well attested; **MEDIUM** = attested, exact wording/figure to verify; **LOW** = secondary sources disagree.

## Known date disputes

- **Butlerian Jihad 201–108 BG** — dates from Herbert's glossary; narrative of the war differs across Herbert / Dune Encyclopedia / Legends of Dune.
- **Harkonnen stewardship start (10111 AG)** — secondary/EU; the novel gives an approximate span only.
- **Messiah dating (10205 vs 10207 AG)** — sources compute differently from '12 years of jihad'. Use 'twelve years after Paul's ascension' on screen.
- **Leto II's death (~13725 AG)** — computed from a ~3,500-year reign; avoid a precise year on screen unless verified.
- **Heretics/Chapterhouse AG years** — from secondary timelines; anchor on screen as '~1,500 years after Leto II'.
- **Dune: Prophecy** — show-specific chronology; never align it to Sisterhood of Dune dates without a label.
