# DUN003: Music and SFX cue sheet (v1, 2026-11-23)

**Timeline:** VO master v2, `DUN003_VO_FULL_paced_v2.mp3` (12:09), with storyboard v4 (programme 12:11). All times are mm:ss on that timeline. There are 2.5 s gaps between chapters, and music carries the transitions.
**Sourcing:** I can't generate music here, so every cue below is a brief for picking tracks from a licensed library.

## Rules for every cue
- **No soundalikes of Hans Zimmer's *Dune* scores**: no throat-singing "Dune" motif, no Zimmer-style bagpipe or vocal calls. Soundalikes trigger Content ID claims and invite comparison. The Fremen/desert colour comes from modal reed, frame drum and wordless vocal instead.
- **Licensing:** only tracks licensed for monetised YouTube use (e.g. Epidemic Sound, Artlist, Musicbed, or the YouTube Audio Library with attribution checked). Record every track in the licence log at the bottom.
- **Mix targets:**

  | Element | Target |
  |---|---|
  | Programme loudness | −14 LUFS integrated |
  | True peak | −1 dBTP |
  | VO | about −16 LUFS short-term |
  | Music bed under VO | about −26 to −22 LUFS |
  | Music in VO-free gaps | up to about −18 LUFS |

  Sidechain-duck music 6–10 dB under VO.
- **Silence is a cue.** Three marked drop-outs (7:20, 8:50, 10:24) matter more than any track.
